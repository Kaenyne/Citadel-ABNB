## 3. Reading the evidence together

**What the residual now is.** The like-for-like pricing residual is the disclosed ex-FX ADR y/y minus the five measured or assumed terms (H's reconstruction, 1Q23 to 2Q26). It ran 2.0 to 3.7 pp in 2023 to 2025 (mean 2.40, sourced arithmetic on disclosed integers) and 4.4 and 4.9 pp in 1Q26 and 2Q26. It is the one line on the card nobody measures, and the v3 rule's whole content is "next quarter's residual is this quarter's". The rule works out of sample because the series is persistent and its turns are rare: it is late by 0.5 to 1.4 pp in five accelerations and early by 1.15 in the one deceleration (3Q24), and it beats "no change in ex-FX" only because it is less late than that (S note point 6). The K line is the only addition that met its bar and it is a mechanics term of 0.17 pp with a coefficient that was imposed, not estimated.

**Why the fee, regional and composition routes all failed to explain it.** Three decompositions of the residual were tried and none of them is the residual. The fee-migration reprice is real and dated (tranche 1 from 27 October 2025, tranche 2 through 13 October 2026) but its arithmetic is small: a payout-neutral host reprice moves the migrated cohort's ADR by 0.5 to 0.8% and the cohort's y/y share was 12 to 22 pp in 4Q25 to 2Q26, so the reprice is worth 0.09 to 0.15 pp of the residual (K2, descriptive), against a step of 2.0 to 2.5 pp. Fitting the step to the cohort share gives a coefficient 16 times the mechanics, and the same share cannot be told apart from the RNPL ramp or a dummy for the migration window at n 14 (K table 2.4). The regional route rebuilds the blended series from four whole-point regional disclosures, two of which exist only from 4Q24, pays a 0.39 pp reconstruction gap the blended rule never pays, and the only proxies with coverage (US lodging prices for NA) are negatively correlated with NA ex-FX ADR and invert the 2026 jump (L). The composition route measured the one term the mix set lacked, the price of listings under a year old relative to incumbents times their share of stays, and found it is 0.0 to 0.2 pp a quarter with the wrong sign for the story (M). What is left is management's own attribution: the product bundle (cancellation redesign, single fee, RNPL) contributed about 1 pp of ADR in 4Q25 and again in 1Q26 by the GBV-minus-nights gap in the letters (sourced, D014 and D032), which is half of each quarter's step over the 2023-25 mean, and the fee reprice is at most 0.1 to 0.6 pp of that 1 pp; the remainder is consistent with RNPL's mix into larger entire homes (K point 4), a mix effect the H decomposition does not measure. The other half of the step, and all of 2Q26's, is unexplained.

**What the 4Q26 residual band means for the trade.** K's scenario table gives the 4Q26 residual as 3.06 (every residual change since 2Q25 is a dated product step that laps at its anniversary), 3.85 (management's roughly 1 pp 4Q25 bundle contribution laps in full), 3.43 (lap plus the tranche-2 reprice at the central coefficient), 4.85 (the v3 rule carried) and 5.22 (the K rule chained), all labelled assumed except the rule values, and the harness has never scored a lap quarter so it cannot rank them. On card v3's other terms the band runs from reported 4Q26 ADR +2.1% ($170.9) at the lap-only residual to +4.2% ($174.6) at the K rule, which is 2.2 pp of ADR, $479mm of 4Q26 GBV and $65mm of revenue at 131.8mm nights (descriptive arithmetic). K's own reading is that the reprice does not lap in 4Q26 but peaks there (the y/y migrated share rises from +21.5 pp in 2Q26 to +75.0 in 4Q26 and does not fall out of the y/y until 4Q27), so the downside in 4Q26 is not the fee lapping; it is the bundle's 4Q25 contribution lapping and the unexplained half of the step mean-reverting. The 5 November print scores the two stories directly: a 3Q26 residual near 5.0 keeps the rule and the 3.1 to 5.2 band; near 3.9 to 4.1 says the lap arithmetic is the better description and the 4Q26 residual should move to 3.1 to 3.8; below 3.6 is J's mean-reversion downside and retires the rule for 4Q26 (score sheet section 5).

**The lap decision.** N adopts WS-D's global lap (case B, 4Q26 nights +8.1%, 131.8mm) as the baseline and keeps the team's 8.9% (132.7mm) as the top of the band. The case rests on letter dates the team's PR #32 contradicts on geography (the cancellation redesign and fee tranche 1 were global from October 2025) plus one assumed split pinned by the 4Q25 "over 200 bp" disclosure. K's dating agrees with D's on the fee side and adds that the fee reprice's ADR effect and the fee's nights effect run on different clocks: the nights lap is a 4Q25 anniversary, the ADR reprice peaks a year later. The revenue difference between the two nights cases is $21mm at card v3's 4Q26 ADR; the nights-rate difference is a full point. Both cases sit inside D's inconclusive band for the 4Q26 guide (7.6 to 9.4%), so the guide number alone will not settle it; the bundle-contribution figure will (N memo 2).

## 4. What this means for the pitch

1. **The decomposition slide, with the one unobserved line labelled.** 3Q26 ex-FX ADR +3.9% (with the K line; +3.7% without) = measured geographic mix -1.43 (E's regional stays split, band -1.57 to -0.94), measured party size +0.80 (booked capacity +1.35% y/y on 2.05mm vintage-matched reviews, band +0.53 to +1.00), measured length of stay +0.06 (band -0.08 to +0.29), assumed new-business dilution -0.48 (band -0.75 to -0.23), descriptive interaction -0.10, assumed fee-migration mechanics +0.17 (band 0.00 to +0.93), and the unobserved like-for-like pricing residual +4.85 (the 2Q26 value; band 2.40 to 4.85). FX -0.43 (midpoint of the euro fit -1.12 and the baskets +0.26). Reported +3.4%, $177.2. Every number on that slide is labelled and the audience can see that the three measured terms net to -0.57, the two fills to -0.58, and everything else is the residual. That is more credible than a point estimate that hides which line carries the forecast.
2. **The ADR risk is one-sided, and larger than the card's central band suggests.** If the residual mean-reverts to its 2023-25 level (2.40) instead of holding at 4.85, 3Q26 reported ADR is about +1.0% ($173.0) instead of +3.4%: 2.45 pp of ADR, $616mm of 3Q26 GBV, $110mm of 3Q26 revenue, about $2.4bn of annualised GBV (descriptive arithmetic on card v3's terms). Nothing measured in this run points the other way: the fee reprice is worth 0.2 pp, the new-listing composition 0.05, the regional carry sits 0.3 to 0.7 pp below the rule. Carry the mean-reversion case as the downside scenario, not as a tail, and say that the harness has never seen a lap quarter.
3. **The v3 result is a claim about the dollar line, not the ex-FX sentence.** Say that the card's residual rule beats "no change" on reported dollar ADR out of sample (ratio 0.87 to 0.92, ten quarters, every drop-one sample below 1) and say in the same breath that on the letter's whole-point ex-FX figure it does not, that the rule was chosen after seeing J3's table and pre-registered on 11 September, and that the binding jackknife margin is 0.011. A judge who checks will find exactly that.
4. **Name the FX estimator: the midpoint of the euro fit and the regional baskets.** It is the best of the three on both the full record (0.406 pp RMSE) and the scored window (0.332), and the 1.4 pp spread between the two single estimators (3Q26: +2.7% on the euro fit, +4.1% on the baskets, with K) remains the largest controllable uncertainty on the ADR line. The 3Q26 print scores all three against the disclosed FX effect.
5. **The 4Q26 nights baseline is 8.1% (131.8mm), with 8.9% (132.7mm) as the top of the band.** Revenue at card v3's 4Q26 ADR is $3,134mm at the baseline and $3,155mm at the top, both above the anticipated guide arithmetic ($3,050 to $3,100mm) and below consensus ($3,200mm). The pitch should present the lap as a nights-growth-rate question, not a revenue-dollar question, and should name the bundle-contribution figure as the disclosure that decides it.
