# Citadel 2026 Intercollegiate Stock Pitch Competition — verified format

Source: https://www.citadel.com/careers/investing/citadel-associate-program/apply-for-2026-intercollegiate-stock-pitch-competition/ (checked 2026-09-01)

- Teams of 2–4 undergraduates; graduating Dec 2026 – May 2029; US/Canada universities.
- Pitch a long or short on one of eight US-listed stocks: ABNB, ADBE, CPRT, GEV, NCLH, NKE, SBUX, SPOT.
- Time horizon: 3–12 months.
- Submission: 2-page max PDF investment memo (including appendix) and a model.
- Interest form closes Sept 18, 2026. Preliminary submissions due **Oct 2, 2026**. Finalists notified Oct 12. Event in NYC **Oct 22–24, 2026** (travel and lodging covered).
- Judges are Citadel investors; judging criteria are not published.
