From the Street’s margin to ours

Adjusted EBITDA margin, step by step: first what our lower revenue does if costs follow the Street’s own plan, then where each of our cost lines differs from that plan.

Margin level Adds to margin Cuts margin Bars in basis points of margin; hover or tab to a bar for EBITDA dollars.

Revenue step. Our revenue (official model: nights × ADR × the Street’s take rate) with the Street’s total costs allowed to fall with revenue at Airbnb’s historical rate (0.36% of costs per 1% of revenue). This is operating deleverage and does not depend on any view of costs.

Cost steps. Our cost lines are the line build the official model uses. No broker or data vendor publishes the Street’s cost lines, only its total, so the Street’s plan is spread across lines at one common growth rate on last year’s base. Each bar shows how far our line sits above (red) or below (blue) that common rate. AI support automation is the build’s fall in support cost per booking in the period; hosting and AI compute includes the AI inference ramp and the hosting share of the Q3 spending step management guided to.
