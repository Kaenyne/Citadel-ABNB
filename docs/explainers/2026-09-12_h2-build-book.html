02The chain

Revenue is booked value times a take rate, and booked value is nights times price

Two identities carry everything. The second one has a lag in it, because Airbnb recognises revenue at check-in, not at booking.

Where each piece comes from

  • Nights from a quarterly model of the H1 run rate plus the product-bundle lap, cross-checked by an alternative-data index built from review dates.
  • ADR from a seven-term decomposition. Three terms are measured from the same review data, two are assumed, one is a dated mechanics line, and one is unobserved.
  • FX from a fitted translation of the euro and regional currency baskets.
  • Revenue from lagged GBV, not from same-quarter GBV, because of recognition timing.

03Nights, 3Q26

Every model lands between 9.5 and 10.3 before the lap, and the baseline is 9.9

The models disagree about the size and timing of the product-bundle lap, not about underlying demand. The guide of "low double digits" sits at the top of the cluster.

How 9.9% was chosen

  • Point: the PR #32 model, built on the four disclosed 2025 to 2026 quarters with fitted product terms. RNPL +2.4 points and fee plus cancellation redesign +2.3 points, lapped in North America only. assumed
  • Band low, 8.5: the H1-to-H2 bridge's half-lap scenario. Band high, 10.3: the WS10 regional build with no lap.
  • H1 2026 run rate: 9.2% and 10.3%, so the H1 mean is 9.7%. The seasonal H1-to-Q3 transition over 2023 to 2025 is -0.3 points. sourced
  • Q3 lap cost: about 0.4 points of total nights, from NA going 7 to 5.6 at a 28.8% share.

The pre-registered threshold

At or above 10.5% is the surprise. 10.0% or below is the pattern. A deceleration against 2Q26's 10.3% is the base case in every model except the naive carry.

04Nights, the alternative-data check

A stays index built from review dates beats a naive forecast out of sample

Reviews are posted 0 to 14 days after check-out, so their dates are a stay-date series. Counting them across 123 Inside Airbnb markets gives a quantity index that maps onto reported nights.

Construction, in order

  • Count reviews by listing and month in 363 dumps, review dates 2018 to 17 August 2026.
  • Correct survivorship. A review month counted in a dump one year later holds 15 to 16% fewer reviews, because listings delist and take their reviews with them. A y/y inside one dump therefore runs about 20 points too high. Comparing the 2026 dump against the market's own 2025 dump removes it. measured
  • Map to nights. OLS of nights y/y on the index over 14 quarters, slope 0.32 nights-points per index-point, r 0.86.
  • Backtest. Walk-forward RMSE 1.48 points against 2.16 for naive last quarter, a ratio of 0.68, across a family of 2,560 cells at 0.65 to 0.75.

Why the revenue gap is small

A full point of 4Q26 nights is about $29mm of revenue at this ADR, so the 0.9mm gap between the cases is $21mm. Both cases sit above the anticipated guide arithmetic of $3,050 to 3,100mm and below the Zacks consensus of $3,200mm. The lap is a growth-rate question, not a revenue-dollar question.

What settles it on 5 November

Not the 4Q guide, which sits inside the inconclusive 7.6 to 9.4% band for both cases. The disclosure that separates them is whether management repeats a quantified bundle contribution for 3Q26: at or below 1.5 points points to case B, at or above 2.5 to case A.

07ADR, the residual and the test

The residual stepped up in 2026, the rule carries it, and the risk is one-sided

The residual ran 0.8 to 3.7 points from 2023 to 2025 and 4.4 and 4.9 in the first half of 2026. The forecast rule is "next quarter's residual is this quarter's". It passed a pre-registered test, by a small margin, and three attempts to explain the step all failed.

The downside case

If the residual reverts to 2.40 instead of holding at 4.85, 3Q26 reported ADR is about +1.0% ($173.0) instead of +3.4%. That is 2.45 points of ADR, $616mm of 3Q26 GBV and $110mm of revenue. Nothing measured points the other way. Carry it as a scenario, not a tail.

10RNPL, the record

Reserve Now Pay Later is one of three products in a bundle, and the bundle laps on three dates

Management has never split the bundle's contribution by feature. The dates below are what the letters, calls and newsroom posts establish. Sourced items are blue, forward anniversaries are violet.

  • Aug 2025
    RNPL launches in the United States
    $0 upfront, payment due shortly before the free-cancellation window closes. US guests, US domestic stays, flexible or moderate policies. About 70% take-up among guests offered it.
  • Oct to Dec 2025
    Cancellation redesign and single fee, global
    New Limited policy with free cancellation to 14 days before check-in. Host-only service fee of 15.5%: property managers from October, most single-fee hosts from December.
  • 12 Feb 2026, 4Q25 call
    "Over 200 basis points of nights, roughly 300 of GBV" from the three features
    Over 70% adoption on eligible GBV. The first quantified bundle figure.
  • 17 Feb to 4 Mar 2026
    RNPL goes worldwide
    United Kingdom 18 February, Australia and Asia Pacific 23 February, Canada 4 March. Excluded by payment currency: BRL, INR, TRY. So the 2025 cohort was United States only, and the ex-NA anniversary is 1Q27.
  • 7 May 2026, 1Q26 letter
    "About 3 points of nights, 4 points of GBV"; RNPL is roughly 20% of GBV booked
    Over 25% of listings on the single fee. Unearned fees will be lower in Q1 and Q2 and higher in Q3.
  • 6 Aug 2026, 2Q26 letter
    The bundle figure is dropped
    RNPL over 20% of GBV, about 50% of listings on the single fee, eligible booking types expanded in July. Implied take rate 13.2%, moved by booking-versus-stay timing.
  • 15 Sep and 13 Oct 2026
    Fee tranche 2 deadlines
    Non-EEA hosts, then EEA and Switzerland. Three to four times the size of tranche 1. The migrated share of nights rises through 4Q26 rather than lapping.
  • 4Q26
    The October 2025 features anniversary
    Cancellation redesign and fee tranche 1 lap everywhere. This is the 0.70 to 0.88 point ex-NA lap in the 4Q26 nights case, and the roughly 1 point 4Q25 bundle contribution to ADR laps with it.
  • 1Q27
    The ex-NA RNPL anniversary, partial quarter
    Go-lives landed in the last five to six weeks of 1Q26, so the full lap arrives from 2Q27.
  • 4Q27
    The fee reprice leaves the ADR y/y
    Migrated share y/y: +12.5 points in 4Q25, +21.5 in 2Q26, +46 in 3Q26, +75 in 4Q26, back to zero in 4Q27.
