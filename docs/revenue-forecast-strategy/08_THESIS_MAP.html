05How to tackle the thesis — in this order

  1. Reframe the object.Lead the memo with the mechanism: a quarter's revenue is mostly booked before it starts, so the guide is arithmetic on printed GBV plus a shrinking cushion. Say plainly there is no level edge on a guided quarter — that sentence buys credibility for everything after it.
  2. Publish the 5 Nov Q4 guide as a distribution, not a call.Midpoint over the GBV3Q26 grid ($25.9–27.0bn), with and without the fee step at θ < 1, against two vendor-stamped anchors. Say first that consensus disagrees with itself; then give P(below) as a range (0.49–0.73), never one number.
  3. Make composition the variant view.Dollar lap (kernel-implied FX is an output; the −3.4pp step double counts), product lap (the RNPL / cancellation / fee bundle, not "+3 points from RNPL"), and the mix identity (geo −1.5pp and deepening; bedroom nights +0.46pp not +2pp; seats −0.5pp). Both narratives on the tape misread the shape.
  4. Put the asymmetry on one line and one date.3Q26 printed take rate ≥ 18.10 % marks FY27 up ~2pp of growth and ~1 turn of multiple; below it the fee thesis is dead and Q4 nights guided "high single digit" is the bear branch. Write the flip rule into the memo so the judges see you would change your mind.
  5. Close the three memo-blocking gaps this week.GBV3Q26 nowcast with a live registered row; θ from the deadline price panels; one reconciled take-rate number. Everything else on the gap map is for finals or for 2027.
  6. Run the kill list and the vintage rule.Strike the withdrawn numbers (−3.4pp FX step, 82 % determined, +4.05 % uplift as measured, 9/9 rule, half-of-ADR-is-unit-size). Every figure names its replay (PIT vs full-sample) and its vendor timestamp — the hindsight premium alone is 0.3–0.9pp.

06The revenue tree — what granular detail actually gets into revenue

Two layers. Layer 2 is check-in dated and converts already-booked GBV into revenue; Layer 1 is booking dated and builds GBV. ADR and take rate appear as output nodes on purpose: they fall out of the identity and are never inputs — treating them as levers is where the double counting came from.
