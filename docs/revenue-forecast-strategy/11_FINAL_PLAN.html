The plan: from booked GBV to a price view, with RNPL in the loop

Five pictures. The chain the pitch runs on, where RNPL enters it, what the pitch says, where each alpha sits, and the work between now and the print.

1The chain — and the three places RNPL touches it

Reserve Now, Pay Later is ~21% of GBV. The guest books with no cash down, so bookings and nights show up in the KPIs before any money does, and they cancel more often.

# RNPL as a variable — how it enters the model
GBV_reported(q) = GBV_paid(q) + GBV_unpaid_RNPL(q) # unpaid share u = 6–16 % of the backlog ($1.6–4.7bn; 7–19M live unpaid nights at 30 Jun 26)
Backlog(q) = unearned_fees + funds_payable (paid) + U(q) (unpaid) # excess unpaid share vs pre-RNPL norm: +2.0 (4Q25) → +8.1 (1Q26) → +9.7pp (2Q26)
Revenue(q) = c_s · Σ_k φ_k · GBV(q−k) · (1 − L(q)) # L = s_RNPL × Δcancel ≈ 0.2–1.4 % → −$8M to −$67M on 3Q26
Nights_reported = Nights_underlying + pull-forward − cancellation tail # 3Q26 +9.3 % (band 8.8–9.8) vs team +9.9 %; 4Q26 +7.6 % vs +8.9 %
λ_Q3(2026) = Revenue_3Q26 ÷ $27,867M # < 17.09 % (rev < $4,761M) warning · < 16.93 % ($4,719M) escalate

Why F is an alpha and not just a risk: the setup already happened — reported GBV and nights ran ~4 points ahead of the paid backlog from 3Q25, the multiple expanded from 13.3x to 18.2x on that growth, and the 10-Q now says those bookings cancel more. The pitch in one line: Airbnb's growth is largely already booked, part of it isn't paid for, and the market is paying 18x for the part that is arithmetic. RNPL is the variable that makes the bear branch real (unpaid share +10 points, higher cancellations, laps in 3Q26 and 1Q27) and the alpha that the 5 Nov print scores first. The kernel is what lets us say which numbers are already determined — and therefore which ones the multiple should not be paying for.
