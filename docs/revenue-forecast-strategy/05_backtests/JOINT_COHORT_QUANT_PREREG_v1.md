## Objective and accounting object

Consolidate two questions through one matrix: how much of recognition-quarter revenue is associated with each earlier booking quarter, and how much recognized revenue is associated with each dollar of a booking-quarter denominator. Define model allocation `A[b,t]`, backward share `S[b,t]=A[b,t]/sum_b A[b,t]`, and effective forward coefficient `C[b,t]=A[b,t]/reported_GBV[b]`. The same matrix must support both identities. Retain the dollar residual `actual_revenue[t]-sum_b A[b,t]` visibly.

Reported GBV includes cancellation/alteration effects in the period they occur and is not original gross bookings. Consequently this is an **effective accounting allocation**, not an observed physical cohort, cancellation probability, survival rate or causal RNPL estimate. Do not add a generic cancellation haircut. Do not infer a missing physical quantity from a fitted coefficient. Every forward row is right-censored after the last available recognition quarter; finite lag support is an assumption.

## Inputs and evidence gate

Read-only frozen corporate KPI panel and publication calendar supply revenue/GBV totals. Data agent audits PR60 and all promising local altdata. Data without original booking and stay/recognition dates cannot independently identify the joint matrix. Same-day printed KPIs are allowed at the after-release guide-date origin; only rows with publication date at or before the origin enter training. Reconstructed present-day alternative-data panels do not become historical point-in-time inputs merely because they carry old event dates.

K2 Melbourne value shares may be used only in a separately labelled, retrospective assumption stress. Restrictions act on recognition-column shares, never directly on forward coefficients. Calendar-month and the original seasonal-analogy mappings are separate assumptions. K2 is dated 2016–2017 accommodation value from one city with failed truncation recovery, not current corporate fee revenue. Its published low/high cells are scenario bounds, not confidence intervals. No K2 or PR60-derived new feature enters the historical predictive fitting rule unless genuine historical publication vintages are demonstrated.

## Pre-registered methods

1. **Flexible identification diagnostic.** `A[b,t]=beta[season(t),t-b]*GBV[b]`, `beta>=0`. Main support is lags0–8; 9+ is unobserved, not proven zero. For W1 (2023Q1–2026Q2, expected n14) and W2 (2024Q1–2026Q2, n10), fit each observed revenue within 1%; repeat 0.5% and 2%, with tail support ending at lag3 and lag4. Publish design rank/parameter count, feasible share and effective-coefficient bounds, and witness allocations. This 36-parameter diagnostic can be saturated; it cannot by itself reject a smaller predictive model. Bounds are conditional sensitivity/identification sets, not sampling confidence intervals.
2. **Parsimonious candidate.** Four nonnegative pooled exposure weights on current GBV, lag1, lag2, and the equal-weight average of lags3 and4; weights sum to one. Four season-specific conversion scales are estimated as same-season exponentially weighted means of `R/(X*w)`, half-life two annual observations. This gives 7 estimated degrees of freedom (3 weight +4 scale). Three deterministic optimizer starts. Minimize weighted squared relative revenue errors; no intercept. Tail3 alone and equal lags3–8 are alternative truncation specifications, not silently pooled evidence. Full-sample W1/W2 fits are retrospective.
3. **Parsimonious identification and parameter uncertainty.** Enumerate a simplex grid at 0.05 increments, retaining shapes whose retrospective relative RMSE is within 0.25 percentage points of the minimum (also include the continuous optimum). Publish absolute fit error, number of shapes, range of implied backward shares and effective coefficients. Keep these model-set widths distinct from 90% percentile year-cluster bootstrap intervals (200 deterministic draws, reject draws missing a season) and leave-one-year/deletion shifts. Few independent years make bootstrap intervals descriptive/conditional, not strong population evidence.
4. **Conditional time variation and covariance.** For the fitted shape, also allocate each realized revenue total using the model's normalized exposure shares. This exact reconciliation is an explicitly assumed allocation, not reconstructed observation. Effective coefficients then use that same dollar matrix. Report within-season n, sample variance/SD/range for backward shares and effective coefficients on both windows, and the full covariance of dollar contributions with `Var(sum)=sum(diagonal)+sum(off-diagonal)` reconciliation. The imposed fixed shape can understate true composition variation.
5. **Point-in-time race.** At the prior quarter's earnings/guide date, train only on already published revenue and GBV, forecast the target quarter's unprinted GBV recursively as the same quarter a year earlier times the latest published GBV year-on-year growth. The candidate never sees target GBV or target revenue while forecasting. Compare with fixed 2/3–1/3 EWM on the same origins, and report a realized-GBV oracle separately. Candidate minimum training is eight complete observations and all four seasons, with required lag prehistory; all skipped W1 origins and reasons are retained. Baseline uses its own admissible history. Repeat tail alternatives with their own clearly disclosed coverage. Report paired revenue and guide RMSE, MAE, bias; both methods use the same trailing-eight actual/issued-guide cushion known at the origin. Delete one evaluation year and one evaluation quarter for score stability. New algorithms are tested by historical replay; no claim that researchers chose them historically.

## Research pass lines, fixed before results

- **Observed-cohort pitch claim:** requires relevant booking/stay/dollar/cancellation definitions plus historical coverage and representativeness; fitted accounting allocations do not satisfy this evidence gate.
- **Quoted estimated share:** both W1/W2 must have same-season identification/near-fit and 90% parameter intervals no wider than 10 percentage points, and maximum leave-one-year mean-share shift no larger than 5 points. These are internal decision thresholds, not statistical laws. A failure in any required evidence or stability gate blocks a headline percentage. Small time variance does not waive identification uncertainty.
- **Forecast promotion:** primary parsimonious candidate guide RMSE must be at most 0.90 times the fixed-kernel baseline in both W1 and W2, with at least eight common origins in each, and ratio must stay below 1 under every evaluable year/quarter deletion. Report failure, not retune. Revenue scores also reported; current-GBV oracle cannot qualify.
- **Integrity:** nonnegative allocations; exact backward/forward algebra; residual and tail/censoring visible; leakage tests; no existing input hash changes; deterministic rebuild. A forecast failure is a successful negative research result, not permission to remove it.
