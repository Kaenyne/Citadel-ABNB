## Essential equations and conservation

Let X=wG1+(1−w)G2, R=lambda X and guide G=R/(1+c). Under w=2/3 the implied first-lag arithmetic share is 2G1/(2G1+G2). If G1/G2 is nearly constant within season, lambda[1+w(r−1)] can compensate for different w. Neither equation supplies a complete booking-cohort allocation.

For attribution only, define X* from eventual printed GBV, lambda*=Ractual/X*, and P*=Gactual/Ractual. Let u=Xhat/X*−1, k=lambdahat/lambda*−1 and p=Phat/P*−1, where Phat=1/(1+chat). Then:

`Ghat − Gactual = Gactual × (u + k + p + uk + up + kp + ukp)`.

The evaluation exports all seven terms and the adjustment from raw to interval error. MSE is the sum of each term's second moment **plus all pair cross moments**. Summed cross terms are −3,042.816/−3,986.798 USDm² in W1/W2; full MSE is 4,025.227/4,057.205. Ordered decompositions allocate interactions by convention, not unique causality. Measurement, fees, FX or RNPL may enter residual conversion error, but that does not identify their separate effects.

Accounting: full booking GBV is recorded regardless of payment timing; revenue recognition is generally at check-in. RNPL changes payment timing; incremental demand, cancellation behavior and revenue-flow magnitude remain unidentified. Unpaid-backlog stocks do not provide revenue-flow weights. Reported USD GBV already embeds currency translation. A future FX adjustment requires compatible booking/fixing/recognition flows and signed hedge attribution; disclosed Q2/H1 revenue hedge losses of $19m/$34m do not identify that future split. See the [Q2 2026 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972026000027/abnb-20260630.htm).

Financial identities: `net income = operating income + net interest − tax`; `FCF = adjusted EBITDA + net interest − cash tax + change in unearned fees + working-capital contribution − capex`; `corporate net cash next = prior net cash + cash generation − repurchases − cash withholding + other specified corporate flows`; `shares next = prior shares + net issuance − shares repurchased`; `value/share = (multiple × EBITDA + corporate net cash)/shares`. A cushion-only change enters none of the actual-revenue financial identities.

## Strongest competing interpretation and measurable falsifiers

The strongest constructive interpretation is that stable conversion, corporate cash generation and modeled share reduction can support the business while revenue expectations already anticipate much of its growth. Conversely, a bearish operating case needs a sufficiently persistent, same-basis earnings shortfall; unsupported RNPL/FX causal magnitudes or a mixed-basis guide subtraction cannot supply it. Neither direction is selected here.

1. **Bookings and conversion:** compare the next printed Q3 GBV with $25,780.297m and the inherited $26,008.556m reference, with the source definition held consistent. The first threshold removes the frozen LSEG gap only at held-fixed lambda and other assumptions. Joint changes require the full equation.
2. **Management policy and expectations:** record the first Q4 guide and separately timestamped guide/revenue expectations. Compare each object with its matching forecast. A new surprise sign can invalidate the frozen comparison; one observation cannot establish a tradeable return mechanism.
3. **Persistence and costs:** distinguish one-quarter timing from repeated revenue differences and observe incremental cash costs, taxes and working capital. The $2.77 sensitivity is not defensible as an investment effect without FY27 persistence.
4. **Cash and shares:** reconcile reported corporate funds, cash withholding, net issuance, repurchased shares and transaction prices. Replace the starting weighted-average proxy with a supported endpoint share count when available.
5. **Model usefulness:** genuinely archived future-origin forecasts must meet the unchanged two-baseline loss/stability rule before reconsidering promotion. A source-date or future-input failure would instead invalidate the historical reconstruction.
6. **Missing mechanisms:** require conserved cohort denominators for RNPL, compatible fixing/recognition/hedge data for FX and completed comparable fee captures for theta. Until then, their empirical magnitude remains unidentified. NCLH transfer remains failed and supplies no adjustment.

The project records a November 5 earnings catalyst after October finals. These schedule dates are retained as project assumptions, not verified current external calendar facts; no monitoring is scheduled. The evidence needed next is specified without inventing future observations.
