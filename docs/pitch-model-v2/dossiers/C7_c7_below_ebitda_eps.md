# C7 — SBC, D&A, interest, tax, share count, EPS: the below-EBITDA bridge

## 1. Header
- Line: C7 · Judge's question: "How do you get from EBITDA to $5.93 and $4.58 of EPS?"
- Digger: sonnet · Date: 2026-09-18 · Commit: e6d9832 (branch `theo/pitch-model-v2`; the brief named b098ac2, an ancestor — `git diff --stat b098ac2 e6d9832` on every file this dossier cites (M7 parameter sheet, `40_line_build`'s outputs, the 02 panel, `capital_return_panel.py`, `DECISIONS.md`) touches only `DECISIONS.md` (+2 lines, DEC-0016/0017, already read below), so nothing this line depends on changed)

**One-paragraph answer.** $5.93 is `40_line_build`'s **base** FY27 EPS; $4.58 is its **short-case** FY27 EPS (`40_short_case_summary.csv`, scenario `short_costs_at_budget`). Both numbers are produced by the *identical* six-line below-EBITDA arithmetic — reused unchanged from `M7_below_ebitda`'s own pre-registered, harness-backtested rules (`40_line_build.md`: "Below EBITDA: D&A $21M/q; SBC +13.2% y/y; interest income 0.876 x 3m T-bill x earning base; interest expense $37M/q; ETR 18%/17.5%; shares -5.3M/q. M7 parameter sheet, reused unchanged.") — applied to two different adjusted-EBITDA/revenue paths: `40_line_build`'s own bottom-up base case (FY27 adj. EBITDA $5,644.2M, 35.7% margin) and its short case (nights-lap + RNPL cost overlays, FY27 adj. EBITDA $4,761.4M, 31.9% margin). The exact identity, read from `analysis/src/margin_build/40_line_build/run.py` lines 180-188 (never executed by this digger — see the hard constraint in §5): **op_income = adj_EBITDA − D&A − SBC; pretax = op_income + interest_income − interest_expense + other_income; net_income = pretax × (1 − ETR); EPS = net_income ÷ diluted_shares_m** (diluted shares walk +delta_per_q from 597.0m at 2Q26; delta = −buyback$/price + net award issuance = −5.32m/quarter). D&A and SBC are themselves scenario-invariant across base/short/breaker (they depend only on the historical panel and a fixed y/y growth rate, never on the revenue path) — the entire $1.35 FY27 EPS spread between $5.93 and $4.58 comes from (a) the different adj. EBITDA feeding `op_income`, and (b) a smaller interest-income base in the short case (RNPL shifts 10pts of GBV to pay later, cutting average funds held ~10%). Below-EBITDA items contribute at most a few cents of that spread; the debate is an EBITDA debate, exactly as `M7_below_ebitda.md` concludes for the Street comparison ("the 5 Nov EPS argument is an EBITDA argument, not a bridge debate").

EBITDA, the bridge to $5.933 EPS removes $82.5M of D&A and $2,052.7M of SBC to get $3,508.9M of GAAP operating income, adds $745.4M of interest income and $3.7M×4 of other income, subtracts $37.0M×4 of interest expense, taxes the $4,120.7M pretax result at 17.5% to get $3,399.9M of net income, and divides by 573.0m average diluted shares. 2. `data/processed/margin_build/02_financial_panel/02_panel_quarterly.csv` → the H1 2026 actuals this line's FY26/FY27 annual formulas fold in: `net_income` (1Q26 $160M, 2Q26 $816M), `pretax_income`/`tax_provision`/`effective_tax_rate_pct` (1Q26 43.06% on $281M, 2Q26 9.03% on $897M), `shares_diluted_m` (608.0m, 597.0m), `funds_held_on_behalf` and `gbv_busd` (the interest-income earning-base inputs), `sbc_total_is` (410, 487) and `da` (22, 17).
3. `analysis/src/margin_build/M7_below_ebitda/run.py` (read only, never executed by this digger — hard constraint) → the pre-registered, harness-backtested rules for `interest_income`, `sbc`, `da`, `tax`, `share_count` and the `eps` waterfall (§6 backtests this exact rule set).
