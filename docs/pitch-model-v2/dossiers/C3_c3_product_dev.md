# C3 — Product development, the line nobody argues about

## 1. Header
- Line: C3 · Judge's question: "What drives this cost line, and what did the 10-Q say about it?"
- Digger: sonnet · Date: 2026-09-18 · Commit: 0b0961e (branch `theo/pitch-model-v2`; the brief named b098ac2, an ancestor — `git diff --stat b098ac2 0b0961e` on every file this dossier cites returns nothing except two appended rows in `docs/pitch-model-v2/DECISIONS.md` (DEC-0016/0017), already read and unrelated to this line)

**One-paragraph answer.** Product development is one thing: payroll from headcount growth, with no per-unit driver and no cyclicality. The line build's formula is `pd_2h26 = (3Q25+4Q25 actual) × (1 + 9.0%)` and `pd_fy27 = (1Q26+2Q26 actual + pd_2h26) × (1 + 8.0%) + $30M`, spread on the 2023–25 mean quarterly shares — no fee rate, no per-booking metric, no hosting schedule, unlike cost of revenue or ops & support. At the guide midpoint this build puts 3Q26 at $359.1M (+7.8% y/y, 7.5% of revenue) and FY27 at $1,625.3M (10.3% of revenue, +10.0% y/y). The 10-Q says exactly this, twice: "Product development expense increased $62 million, or 10% … primarily due to a $62 million increase in payroll-related expenses resulting from an increase in average headcount" (2Q26 vs 2Q25), and $132M/+11% on the same basis for 1H26 (2Q26 10-Q, MD&A). What makes this line different from every other cost line in the margin build: it receives **none** of the $97M 3Q26 sentence-reconciliation gap (100% goes to marketing and hosting, 0% to PD), it carries **no backcast of any kind** (unlike cost of revenue's two-point backcast), it is **not mentioned anywhere in memo v3**, and the one pre-registered test built on this exact panel column — M6's revenue-cyclicality regression (T1) — fails with the **wrong sign** (k = −0.21, t = −1.59 at lag 0, significantly negative at t = −3.25 at lag 2), while the harsher, correctly-specified drift-baseline re-score (R14) shows the best driver-trend model of this line loses to seasonal-naive-drift in **both** windows (ratio 1.22 W1, 1.28 W2). Product development is a headcount budget, not a forecast, and the repo's own evidence base for it is the thinnest of any line in this batch.

## 3. Derivation chain
1. Filing facts → 2Q26 10-Q MD&A: "Product development expense increased $62 million, or 10%, primarily due to a $62 million increase in payroll-related expenses resulting from an increase in average headcount" (3 months) and "$132 million, or 11% … increase in payroll-related expenses resulting from an increase in average headcount" (6 months); 2Q26 earnings call, "headcount growth below FY25's +12%" and the CEO's "material increase … in terms of the AI spend" (the same sentence that seeds cost of revenue's hosting step, but which the line build assigns **zero** dollars to product development — see `40_params.csv` row `pd_ai_tooling_fy27`'s note: "the engineering-tools part," a judgement split management never itemised). All quoted with source in `data/processed/margin_build/40_line_build/40_params.csv` (rows `pd|*`).
2. `data/processed/margin_build/02_financial_panel/02_panel_quarterly.csv` → `pd_cash` = GAAP product development less product-development SBC; independently checked against `data/processed/abnb_quarterly_costlines.csv` (GAAP `product_development_musd`: 1Q26 $638M, 2Q26 $672M) and the 2Q26 10-Q's own SBC table (product development SBC $298M of $487M total, 2Q26; $561M of $897M, 1H26): $672M − $298M = $374M = the committed `pd_cash` cell exactly; 3Q25 $333M, 4Q25 $335M.
3. `analysis/src/margin_build/40_line_build/run.py` lines 32–33 (`SHARE["pd"]` = {Q1 .2548, Q2 .2466, Q3 .2459, Q4 .2527}, 2023–25 mean quarterly shares, WS02 `02_seasonality.csv`), 60–62 (parameters), 124–125 (`pd_2h26 = (pan["3Q25"]+pan["4Q25"]) × (1+pd_growth_2h26/100)`; `pd_fy27 = (pan["1Q26"]+pan["2Q26"]+pd_2h26) × (1+pd_growth_fy27/100) + pd_ai_tooling_fy27`), 171 (`pdv = pd_fy27 × SHARE[qn]` for 2027 quarters, `pd_2h26 × SHARE[qn]/H2["pd"]` for 2026 quarters, `H2 = SHARE[3]+SHARE[4] = 0.4986`). No revenue, GBV, nights or bookings term enters this line anywhere — the only three inputs are two actual half-year totals and two growth rates plus one FY27 dollar add.
4. `data/processed/margin_build/40_line_build/40_lines_quarterly.csv`, column `pd_cash`, rows `3Q26…4Q27` × scenario; `40_annual.csv` same column for FY26/FY27; `40_short_case_quarterly.csv` for the short case (numerically identical to `base`).

Independent re-derivation (this dossier, read-only): `data/processed/pitch_model_v2/receipts/C3/c3_pd_recompute.py` reads `40_params.csv` and the actual `pd_cash` cells directly and recomputes `pd_2h26`, `pd_fy27` and all six quarterly + two annual cells for `base`, `cost_bull` (breaker) and the short case; all 22 cells match the committed CSVs to max abs diff **5.68e-14** (`c3_pd_recompute_check.csv`).

A different object, same panel column (`pd_cash_musd`): `analysis/src/margin_build/M1_driver_lines/` (own-trend y/y model, spec `b_elastic_rw`) and `analysis/src/margin_build/M6_cycle_flex/` (revenue-elasticity spec, `k_pd` = −0.211) both fit and score this column in `data/processed/margin_build/10_harness_margin/scoreboard_margin.csv` and `data/processed/margin_build/22_discussion_group_A/groupA_paired_vs_drift.csv`; a third, non-forecasting signal — open engineering roles on careers.airbnb.com (`docs/margin-build/notes/04_alt_signals.md` §1.3) — correlates with this same column (r +0.90 lead 0, +0.96 lead 2, n = 6–7) but is "logged, not promising" on the pre-registered n ≥ 14 rule.
