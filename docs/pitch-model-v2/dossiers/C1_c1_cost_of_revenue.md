# C1 — Cost of revenue, the one cost line with a real backtest

## 1. Header
- Line: C1 · Judge's question: "What drives this cost line, and what did the 10-Q say about it?"
- Digger: sonnet · Date: 2026-09-18 · Commit: 11b3d39 (branch `theo/pitch-model-v2`; the brief named b098ac2, an ancestor — `git diff --stat b098ac2 11b3d39` on every file cited below returns nothing, i.e. unchanged)

**One-paragraph answer.** Cost of revenue is four named things: merchant/payment-processing fees (1.70% of GBV, seasonally adjusted, "higher payment processor rebates and incentives" per the 1Q26 10-Q), chargebacks ($0.72 per booking, up from $0.47 in FY25 after two 10-Qs of MD&A dollar deltas), hosting/infrastructure ($224M/yr FY25 run-rate off the FY24 10-K's $672M-through-2027 commitment, re-cut to $1.7bn-through-2031 in the FY25 10-K, plus a 2H26 AI step management calls "material"), and a small per-night residual ($0.36, mostly AirCover claims and payment operations). At the guide midpoint this build puts 3Q26 at $640.8M (+16.7% y/y, 13.3% of revenue) and FY27 at $2,759.6M (17.4% of revenue, +13.4% y/y), of which $100.2M/quarter of FY26 hosting and $111.7M/quarter of FY27 hosting is a management-language reconciliation step, not a disclosed number — the single least-sourced input in the whole line build. What makes this line different from every other cost line in the margin build: it is the **only** one with a real, both-windows-significant backtest. A GBV/booking-elasticity version of this same panel column (`cor_cash_musd`) beats a seasonal-naive-**drift** baseline at p = 0.0004 in W1 (13 of 14 quarters) and p = 0.008 in W2 (9 of 10) — the sole line-level survivor of the harshest test applied to the whole cost stack (S&M, product development and total cash costs all fail it). The line build's own bottom-up formula has not itself been run through that scorer; its own evidence is a backcast (FY25 error −1.0%, 1H26 error +0.8%), which is consistent with, but weaker than, the driver-elasticity result.

## 3. Derivation chain
1. Filing facts → FY24 10-K payment-processing rate (1.69% of GBV) and hosting commitment ("at least $672 million for vendor services through 2027"); FY25 10-K re-cut hosting commitment ("at least $1.7 billion … through 2031", purchase-obligations table $1,749M, <1yr $219M / 1–3yr $930M ≈ $465M/yr in 2027–28); 1Q26 10-Q "higher payment processor rebates and incentives" cutting the annual-equivalent fee rate to ≈1.68%; 1H26 10-Q MD&A chargeback dollar deltas (+$12M, +$13M y/y); 2Q26 10-Q "higher amortization related to reserved instance purchases and increased infrastructure spend" and the CEO's 2Q26 call language on "a material increase … in terms of the AI spend"; 6 Aug 2026 guide ($4,690–4,770M) and "adjusted EBITDA margin down slightly vs 3Q25." All quoted with source in `data/processed/margin_build/40_line_build/40_params.csv` (rows `cor|*`, `recon|*`).
2. `data/processed/margin_build/02_financial_panel/02_panel_quarterly.csv` → `cor_cash` = GAAP cost of revenue (Airbnb has allocated **no SBC** to cost of revenue in any of the 14 quarters 1Q23–2Q26, so `cor_cash` = `cor_gaap` exactly — confirmed independently in the H0 dossier, §5); 1Q26 $581M, 2Q26 $633M.

Independent re-derivation (this dossier, read-only): `data/processed/pitch_model_v2/receipts/C1/c1_cor_recompute.py` reads `40_params.csv` directly and recomputes `cor_fees`, `cor_chargebacks` and `cor_other` for every base and cost_bull quarter from the named parameters and the committed `merchant_fee_rate_q_pct`/`gbv_busd`/`nights_m` cells; all 12 cells match the committed `40_lines_quarterly.csv` values to < 6e-14 (`c1_cor_recompute_check.csv`). `cor_hosting`'s reconciliation-gap term was not independently re-derived (it depends on the full 3Q26 evidence-cost solve across all five lines); the run's own internal assertion (`run.py` line 326: `|cor_cash − (fees+chargebacks+hosting+other)| < 1e-6`) and the Codex/Astra audit's independent reproduction of the reconciliation allocation (finding table, below) stand in its place.

Backtest chain (a different object, same panel column `cor_cash_musd`): `analysis/src/margin_build/M1_driver_lines/` (GBV-elasticity spec, `b_elastic`, exponent 0.81) and `analysis/src/margin_build/M6_cycle_flex/` (revenue-elasticity spec, `k_cor` = 0.562) both fit and score this column in `data/processed/margin_build/10_harness_margin/scoreboard_margin.csv` and `data/processed/margin_build/22_discussion_group_A/groupA_paired_vs_drift.csv`.
