### Conventions adopted (the question's gates are unions and intersections of other questions' resolutions; each is fixed here so 5 Nov resolves mechanically)
1. **Print gates** are on the letter's printed 3Q26 Nights and Seats Booked y/y rate to one decimal on the 133.6m base: "≥10.6%" = latent ≥ 10.5913 (R02's 147.75m threshold); "≤8.5%" = latent < 8.55 (prints 145.0m or less, rate rounds to 8.5% or lower). S01's dead band (decelerating < 10.09, flat 10.09–10.59, accelerating ≥ 10.59) is used for the return cells **and, from revision 2, for the base option's own gate** (convention 4); the 0.0013-point gap between 10.59 and 10.5913 is empty in practice (mass < 0.0003).
2. **Thesis breaker** = printed nights ≥ 10.6% AND C02 resolves (a) ("low double digits" or language implying ≥10%). Both conditions required; "or better" is inside C02 (a).
3. **Short case** = printed nights ≤ 8.5% OR C02 resolves (d) OR C04 resolves (d) — the **literal** (option-level) reading, and the headline. Revision 1 attributed this choice to "the A19 brief"; the orchestrator's launch prompt is now on disk (`docs/pitch-forecasts/prompts/forecast_A19.md`) and says only that the legs are operationalised at the option level, so the reading is defended here from the **question text** (A19-02):

   - The registry's own header convention defines bucket language as "the letter's or call's qualitative growth descriptor for the next quarter", which covers a directional "moderate / decelerate" sentence.
   - (a), (b) and (c) are applied as whole options in conventions 2 and 4; splitting only (d) would be asymmetric.
   - **Against**: C02 (d)'s second clause is expressly "without a bucket", while the X01 text calls the object a "bucket". The clause is genuinely ambiguous; the option-level reading is adopted because it is the one a 5 Nov resolver can apply mechanically from C02's resolution, and the **material** reading (C02 leg = the explicit mid-single-or-lower bucket only) is published beside it in §6–§7 with its own vector. 4. **Base** = printed nights **decelerating** (latent < 10.09, S01 revision 2's dead-band line on 2Q26's 10.34%) AND (C02 ∈ {(b), (c)} OR C01 = Yes, guide midpoint below the LSEG-family Street mean) AND not short case. **Revision 2 correction (A19-01):** revision 1 gated this option at "print < 10.6%", which is the *breaker* threshold, and therefore classified the whole flat/in-line band (10.09–10.59, mass 0.1034) as base whenever C02 ∈ {b,c} or C01 = Yes. The question reserves that band for "none of the above" ("in-line print with in-line guide"); 0.0514 of probability moves from base to none.
5. **None of the above** = every other joint outcome. From revision 2 it contains (i) an accelerating print (≥10.6%) whose descriptor is not (a) and which trips no short gate, 0.065; (ii) a **flat/in-line print (10.09–10.59)** with no short gate, 0.059; (iii) a decelerating print with C02 (a) or (e) and the guide at/above Street, 0.011. "None" is therefore **not** a benign cell: 0.48 of it is an accelerating print and 0.44 an in-line one, and P(C01 below | none) is 0.54.
6. **Precedence** where two options' conditions both hold: thesis breaker > short case > base > none. Short beats base by construction (base excludes short). Breaker vs short can overlap only through C04 (d) on an accelerating "low double digits" print (mass 0.0326). **The question text does not settle this and neither does any repo document (A19-07): this is a stated convention, not a derivation.** Revision 1 defended it with a return statistic (the S01 cell for that print is positive), which is an argument about *reaction*, not about *classification*; that defence is withdrawn. 7. If the letter gives no 4Q26 nights descriptor (C02 (e), 0.04) the breaker cannot resolve; the draw falls to base (if the print is decelerating and C01 = Yes) or none. If no FY26 margin sentence (C04 (e)) the C04 leg is simply not tripped.
