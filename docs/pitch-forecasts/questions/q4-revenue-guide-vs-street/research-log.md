## 0b. Question (verbatim)
### Title
Will Airbnb's 4Q26 revenue guidance midpoint, given at the 5 Nov print, be below the Street's 4Q26 revenue consensus mean?
### Resolution Criteria
Yes if the midpoint of the 4Q26 revenue range in the 3Q26 shareholder letter is strictly below the LSEG-family 4Q26 revenue consensus mean recorded on 4 Nov 2026 (register fallback: Yahoo/LSEG mean captured that day). If only a growth-rate range is given, convert on 4Q25 revenue $2,778M. If no 4Q26 revenue guidance is given, resolves No.
### Fine Print
The consensus used is the revenue mean, not the Street's implied guide. Note in the log the separate P(midpoint below Street minus the trailing-8 cushion), which is the "guide surprise" object, but the headline resolves on the raw mean. Resolution date 5 Nov 2026.

Conventions adopted for ambiguities (registry conventions apply: "Street" = LSEG-family mean at the close of the last trading day before the print, from `L0_vintage_register.csv`, fallback the Yahoo/LSEG mean captured that day): (1) "midpoint" = arithmetic mean of the two dollar endpoints as printed ($X.XX bn, so the midpoint sits on a $5M grid); (2) if the letter prints both a dollar range and a growth range, the dollar range governs; (3) the Street mean is used unrounded to the dollar, so a tie is a measure-zero event; (4) the Zacks $3,200M panel is not the resolution source and is carried only as a sensitivity.
