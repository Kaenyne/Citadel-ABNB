### Fine Print

Conventions adopted: (1) "closing price" = the Nasdaq official close of ABNB on Tuesday 15 Dec 2026 as reported by yfinance `Close` (ABNB pays no dividend, so adjusted and unadjusted closes coincide); (2) if 15 Dec were not a trading session, the last close before it; (3) thresholds are inclusive as written (≤ $150.00, ≤ $143.00, ≥ $180.00); (4) the forecast is unconditional on the 5 Nov print — the print is inside the mixture, not a conditioning event; (5) the 5 Nov day-1 mixture is built here from the same panel as S01 with the print-state weights adopted after S01 revision 1 (R01 0.42 = P(3Q26 nights ≥ +10.0%), R02 0.32 = P(≥ +10.6%), S01's P(guide below Street | decelerating) 0.78), and its unconditional sd is set to S01's 9.5% by the variance identity (section 5); S01 revision 1 (mean −2.7%, median −2.9%, sd 9.5%, P(≤ −8%) 0.29, P(≥ +5%) 0.20) used P(accel) 0.24 — this log's mixture reads mean −1.8%, median −1.9%, P(≤ −8%) 0.26, P(≥ +5%) 0.24, and the whole difference is the R02 weight (sensitivity row "revision-1 print weights"); (6) the question asks for the 15 Dec close of ABNB as listed; a corporate action that ends the listing before 15 Dec (takeover close, delisting) is a resolution case the registry does not define, it is not modelled and no substitute price is asserted (revision 1's "last comparable close" fallback is withdrawn, A07-02); the probability of such an event inside the window is small (no reported approach, ~$100bn market cap, founder control) and is not a separate component of the distribution.
