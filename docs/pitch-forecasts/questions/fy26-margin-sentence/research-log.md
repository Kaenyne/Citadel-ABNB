### Title
What FY26 adjusted EBITDA margin guidance will Airbnb give at the 5 Nov print?
### Resolution Criteria
Multiple choice. Options. (a) held: "at least 35.5%" unchanged; (b) "approximately 36%" or "at least 36%" (35.75–36.24 implied); (c) ≥36.5% or "approximately 36.5%"/higher; (d) any lower or softer language (<35.5% floor, "approximately 35.5%", floor removed); (e) no FY26 margin guidance.
### Fine Print
The margin-build M3 rule (November sentence = numeric floor + 50bp, exact 2 of 2) is one input; base rates from the guidance ledger are another. Resolution date 5 Nov 2026.

Conventions adopted for ambiguous forms (stated here, not changing the question): a range resolves on its midpoint into the option's implied band ("35.5% to 36%" → 35.75 → (b); "35.5% to 36.5%" → 36.0 → (b)); "at least 35.75%" → (b); "approximately 35.5%" → (d) per the option text even though it is not a lowering of the level; "at least 35.5%" restated with a qualifier that keeps the floor → (a); an FY26 adjusted EBITDA dollar guide with no margin figure → (e); letter governs over the call (QUESTIONS.md conventions). "5 Nov print" = the 3Q26 release on its actual date (Investing.com currently projects 4 Nov; the company has not announced).
