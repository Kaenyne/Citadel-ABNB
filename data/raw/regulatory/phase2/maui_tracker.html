How a property moves

  1. STEP 1Housing & Land Use CommitteeCommittee takes testimony, amends, and votes whether to send the resolution forward.
  2. STEP 2Full County CouncilThe whole Council votes on the resolution as it left committee.
  3. STEP 3Planning CommissionsReferred out for review and recommendation — a 120-day window.
  4. STEP 4Back to CouncilReturns with the Commissions' recommendations for final action.

What this is. The Minatoya list identifies apartment-zoned properties that have operated as transient vacation rentals. The County Council is now moving those properties through rezoning resolutions, in batches. Which resolution a building lands in determines how far along it is — and right now that ranges from already through the full Council to not proposed at all.

What this is not. Nothing here predicts how any resolution will end. These are pending legislative actions and they can be amended, deferred, or defeated at any stage.
