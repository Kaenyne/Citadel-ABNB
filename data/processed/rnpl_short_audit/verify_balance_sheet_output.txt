      For B=1.10 to be true with u=15%, BOTH lines would have to be 10% longer-dated while

  (2) The issuer states the cancellation fact directly: RNPL bookings 'have experienced

  Net: once the migration term is removed, there is very little gap left to explain.  A B of

C5 - marginal ADR of bundle-driven bookings
====================================================================================================
Note: '1Q26 ~4pts GBV on ~3pts nights -> 4/3 = 1.33x'; '4Q25 ~300bp on >200bp -> 1.5x'.

Arithmetic.  If RNPL adds a pts to GBV growth and b pts to nights growth, then
  dGBV / dNights = (a * GBV_{t-1}) / (b * Nights_{t-1}) = (a/b) * ADR_{t-1}.
So 4/3 is the marginal ADR relative to the PRIOR-YEAR average ADR, not the current one.
  4Q25: ADR $167.51 vs 4Q24 $158.13 (+5.9%).
        4/3 x prior-year ADR = $210.84 = 1.26x the CURRENT quarter ADR.
  1Q26: ADR $186.82 vs 1Q25 $171.34 (+9.0%).
        4/3 x prior-year ADR = $228.45 = 1.22x the CURRENT quarter ADR.
  2Q26: ADR $183.73 vs 2Q25 $174.48 (+5.3%).
        4/3 x prior-year ADR = $232.64 = 1.27x the CURRENT quarter ADR.

  1Q26: 4/3 = 1.333 relative to 1Q25 ADR; relative to the 1Q26 ADR it is 1.22x.  The note uses 1.33x against the
  quarter's own ADR to convert unpaid GBV into nights, which overstates the ADR and therefore
  UNDERSTATES unpaid nights by ~8%.

  4Q25: the source is '~300bp on >200bp'.  '>200bp' is a LOWER bound on the denominator, so
  300/200 = 1.5 is an UPPER bound on the ratio, not a point estimate.  At 250bp the ratio is
  1.20; at 290bp it is 1.03.  Quoting 1.5x as a derived parameter is not supported.

  Interpretation.  The ratio is the average value of the INCREMENTAL bookings only if the
  'points of GBV' attribution is pure incremental volume.  The 2Q26 10-Q says 'The increase
  in ADR was driven in part by the continued adoption of RNPL', i.e. RNPL raises the ADR of
  the book it touches, which at a 20-21% GBV share includes re-mix of bookings that would
  have happened anyway.  Any such re-mix sits in the GBV numerator with no nights in the
  denominator, inflating the ratio.  4/3 is an UPPER bound on marginal ADR, not a point.

  Downstream: note para 7 says '21% GBV share and 1.33x -> RNPL nights share 16.7%'.
    at 1.15x vs non-RNPL ADR, RNPL nights share = 18.8%
    at 1.20x vs non-RNPL ADR, RNPL nights share = 18.1%
    at 1.33x vs non-RNPL ADR, RNPL nights share = 16.6%
    at 1.50x vs non-RNPL ADR, RNPL nights share = 15.1%
  0.21/1.333 = 15.8% if 1.33x is against the OVERALL average; 16.7% only if it is against the
  NON-RNPL average.  The note does not say which; both are defensible, the label is not.

    quantity alongside a B sensitivity; in fact m is a pure function of the observed UF/FP
    ratio and the assumed guest-fee coefficient, and carries no information the ratio in
    C2e does not already carry - including the FX contamination.
