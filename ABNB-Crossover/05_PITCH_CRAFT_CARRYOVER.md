# Pitch-craft carryover — the judge-tested rules that apply to ABNB regardless of thesis

Distilled from `context/SELECTION_PRINCIPLES.md`, `context/audit_reports/03_PITCH_COMPETITION_PLAYBOOK.md`, `SIG/research/deck_skeleton.md` Parts A–B, and the SIG pitch-mining post-mortems. All of this is competition-generic; none of it is jewelry. Open the source file for the underlying counts and quotes.

## 1. What the room rewards (measured)

- **Alt data wins rooms.** The APH long lost to a BIRK pitch on "easier story, more interesting alt data" despite APH being the better investment. ABNB is a mega-cap consensus name, so the alt-data layer is not decoration; it is the only available edge. (`SELECTION_PRINCIPLES.md` §1, §7)
- **Consumer-facing, ≤2 segments** is the measured winner profile (40% of flagship winners vs 14% of the universe; 73% ≤2 segments). ABNB fits on both.
- **Cap band caution:** winners cluster \$1–15B. ABNB is far above it. The "neglect" leg (SIG: 9 analysts, "under-covered") is unavailable; the pitch must win on mechanism and measurement. Notre Dame's rule: nobody wins pitching a consensus name with a formed opinion — so the ABNB variant view has to be genuinely counter-consensus and provable. (`deck_skeleton.md` B.0, B.3)
- **Upside norm +20% to +45%.** Do not stretch. A world title was won on +6.5%.

## 2. Structure norms (from 18 primary Sohn long decks + BAM winners)

- 12–15 main slides, one argument per slide, every title a declarative sentence; a one-slide "60-second version" appears in 14 of 18.
- **Three pillars, each titled as a correction of a named market belief** (mode = 3).
- "Why cheap" = a **named peer-multiple gap** (8 of 18); a **drawdown is a pillar in 0 of 18**.
- **"Margins depressed by management choice, recovery not yet in the numbers"** is the most common long mechanism (6 of 18). Segment/mix disconnect (SOTP) is in 9 of 18.
- Valuation as a **decomposed multi-year IRR** (growth + FCF yield + multiple + revaluation), DCF in the appendix as table stakes; bear/base/bull with **drivers** varied, not the discount rate; probability-weighted target. Football field: appendix at most.
- **Catalyst = corporate action / contract date / regulation** (9 of 18); an earnings print is the catalyst in exactly one deck, and it is a short. For ABNB, the Q3 print is one of 3–4 dated catalysts, never the only one.
- Short interest is never a thesis pillar in any winning telling.

## 3. Proof standards for a consumer pitch (from CAKE, VITL, EVER, FRPT, WING, BURL)

1. **A growth number is never a single number.** Decompose nights × ADR × take rate × FX, the way CAKE decomposed a comp into traffic × mix × price.
2. **Nominal is presumed guilty.** Show ADR real (deflate by CPI lodging or the BEA accommodations price index in `data/`).
3. **Alt data must be a spread or a mechanism, not a nowcast.** VITL's winning argument was a spread widening. SIG's own Tenoris verdict: 7/10 mechanism, 3/10 nowcast.
4. **Every primary-research claim carries n, date, and role.** "86/100 store owners"; "29 agents across CA/TX/FL/NY, 76%". For ABNB: "n hosts on r/airbnb_hosts, month, city."
5. **A named comparator running the other playbook** (CAKE used Chili's; BURL used Ross and TJX). ABNB: BKNG or Vrbo.
6. **Management quotes are corroboration, not decoration**; a quote that concedes your point beats a chart.
7. **Quantified, not listed, risks.** VITL answered flu recurrence with a Poisson model → 1.7%.

## 4. Q&A patterns (counted over ~45 verbatim judge questions)

1. **Timing / trigger is the modal question**: "What in guidance or consensus has to change, and when?" Have the dated answer.
2. **"Where are you catastrophically wrong"**, including takeout/competitive-response risk.
3. **Normalized FCF floor**: "they fail, they cut capex — what's normal FCF?" For ABNB: FCF after SBC.
4. Management incentives, growth financing, mix shift, unit economics, pricing power.
5. Exactly one question in ~45 concerned valuation methodology.
6. **The origin story**: "how did you come up with this?" Manufacture the physical check (SIG: the Oaks Mall walk). ABNB: hosting or booking experiment, host interviews, a city registry pull.
7. Ackman's magnitude bar for shorts: 50% earnings misses, not 9%.

## 5. Rules that survived every case in the SIG pitch-mining corpus

- **The market hunts the disqualifier and weights it above the aggregate** (Gap: Old Navy −4% erased a re-rate; AEO −13.9% on a beat; ULTA −4% on a beat-and-raise via a small banner). Pre-identify ABNB's disqualifiers (Experiences/Services losses? take-rate direction? a region decelerating?) and pre-frame them on a slide, not in Q&A.
- **The market prices the guide, not the print.** Every move studied was decided by forward guidance or a forward risk.
- **Famous investors exit before the proof lands**; do not invoke smart-money authority — present the evidence sequence.
- **Balance-sheet cleanliness is necessary, not sufficient, for a re-rate** (the 2017 SumZero short's kill-switch happened and the stock still fell 65%). Net cash + buyback is a multiplier, not a thesis.
- **Stale data is a judged failure mode.** Refresh every perishable number through the last print before submitting.
- **Never cite an alt-data vendor uncritically** (Blotnick #13). State the panel's blind spot before a judge does.

## 6. Process habits that paid off on SIG

- **Corrections ledger from day one.** SIG accumulated 33 corrections; the legacy briefs carried ~16 falsified claims that would have been kill-shots. Keep it in the master synthesis and grep the deck against it before every version.
- **Every number source-tagged to `file:line` or filing.** Judges pre-read the deck and are told to ask hard questions.
- **Pair every big reference document with a short decision-organized digest** (Obsidian convention; `DATA_DIGEST` over `DATA_INVENTORY`). Escape dollar signs.
- **Pre-register predictions before the print** (S12 card) and put a tracker on every falsifier (S14). Dated, checkable calls beat undated theses in the corpus.
- **Verify against primary law/filings, not press** (tariff folklore corrected against the Federal Register; VIC fiscal years re-labeled against the 10-K; a 13F filing-agent error caught by reading the XML).
- **Log what was checked and closed** (no Signet apps; Census series suppressed; Bain report discontinued). A recorded negative is worth something in diligence and saves the next person a day.
