# Nashville, New Orleans and Mexico City: original-13 extension

As of September 7, 2026. This adds official supply evidence for the three assigned markets. It does not add Airbnb booking observations or establish independent/boutique eligibility. **Independent rooms remain missing in all three markets.**

| Market | Preferred observation | Geography and vintage | Property-level data actually obtained |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nashville | **42,156 hotel rooms**, observed agency aggregate | Davidson County; CVB page updated September 19, 2025, precise stock date unstated | None; public inspection portal returned 403 |
| New Orleans | **220 hotel/motel licenses**, observed administrative aggregate | City of New Orleans, March 2025 | Historical Revenue CSV: **262 hotel/motel business records**, not unique hotels; 243 exact address strings; no room field |
| Mexico City | **850 hotels / 62,279 rooms**, observed agency aggregate | Ciudad de México, September 30, 2025 | No individual properties; borough/category tables are aggregate bins |

Nashville retains the existing county denominator from [NCVC statistics](https://www.visitmusiccity.com/about/statistics). A newer municipal [preliminary official statement, PDF page 118 / appendix B-54](https://nashville.legistar.com/View.ashx?GUID=CE2707E4-F3CA-4921-A1FD-DE9A7073BF27&ID=15150231&M=F) reports **528 hotels and 61,358 rooms in the Nashville MSA in December 2025** and supplies 2015–2024 MSA room and occupancy history. These are a larger geography, not an update to the county figure. The document attributes its series to NCVC, so municipal republication does not create independent corroboration. Its form is preliminary, not a finalized audited hotel census. Development-pipeline reports were not treated as currently operating inventory.

For New Orleans, the newer [city lodging study](https://nola.gov/nola/media/City-Planning/Major%20Projects/Inclusionary%20Housing%20Study/10-29-2025-ALL-TransientLodgingStudyReport.pdf), revised October 28, 2025, gives the usable citywide administrative aggregate. Appendix A table 2, PDF page 137 / printed page 9, separates **220 hotel/motel licenses, 3 other traveler accommodations and 109 B&B licenses**. The main narrative loosely calls the broader 223 hotels/motels; the detailed table controls our definition. A footnote says the STR Administration's records use different methods/categories from Revenue. No room total is supplied. [New Orleans & Company's meeting page](https://www.neworleans.com/meeting-planners/) separately reports **26,000 rooms within 2.5 miles of downtown**; its [hotels page](https://www.neworleans.com/hotels/) says more than 26,000 downtown. This remains a rounded, partial-area alternate, not citywide capacity and not matched to the license frame.

The downloadable [Revenue dataset ns2v-hzec](https://data.nola.gov/d/ns2v-hzec) contains **391 lodging business records**, including the 262 hotel/motel rows, 1 casino-hotel row, 113 B&B-category rows, 12 rooming/boarding rows and 3 other traveler accommodations. Metadata's last row update is July 16, 2024; a “monthly” refresh label does not make this a current registry. The hotel/motel subset has 18 repeated-address groups, with operator/name changes, dual brands, and even a bar/garage registration. We preserve these records for future entity matching; neither business ID nor address is a reliable physical-hotel key without review. The difference versus the 2025 study is **not evidence of closures**. Dates are business start dates, not Airbnb onboarding dates.

Mexico City's [tourism report, official Congress-hosted PDF](https://www.congresocdmx.gob.mx/media/documentos/b5c1ff207166ed571562c1cc60deeb9dcc220e83.pdf) upgrades the earlier 66,000-room search snippet. Table 8, PDF page 264 / printed page 117, supplies the exact September 2025 total and borough breakdown; its 15 displayed boroughs sum to 850 hotels and 62,279 rooms. Milpa Alta is absent from the table; we do not invent a zero. The category table on PDF page 176 describes the same stock and is not another sample. The history on PDF page 251 explicitly warns that catalog updates added previously omitted hotels, making earlier years non-comparable. Stars do not establish independence or boutique status. Conflicting search-only figures, including 62,729, were not averaged or promoted over the downloaded detailed table.

Source overlap is bounded to the saved visible team URL inventory. The county Nashville anchor is explicitly reused. The New Orleans partial room anchor is the same publisher/assertion as existing work, with clearer geography. No distinctive URL/dataset-ID match was found for the newer municipal statement, New Orleans Revenue file/study, or Congress report. This is not a claim to know what exists in unshared private work. Mexico City remains the same tourism-agency source family as the earlier lead; only one stock figure is used.

Deliverables are [market_results.json](../../data/raw/hotel_13_market_extension/south_mexico/market_results.json), [source ledger](../sources/hotel_13_south_mexico_sources.json), [New Orleans row audit](../../data/raw/hotel_13_market_extension/south_mexico/nola_registry_summary.json), [Mexico tables](../../data/raw/hotel_13_market_extension/south_mexico/mexico_capacity_tables.json), [capacity history](../../data/raw/hotel_13_market_extension/south_mexico/capacity_history.json), and [access gaps](../../data/raw/hotel_13_market_extension/south_mexico/access_gaps.json). The source manifest preserves seven saved source files and nine unsuccessful direct-download attempts. The saved PDFs and web-reader outputs distinguish actual access from search leads.

Run `.venv/Scripts/python.exe data/raw/hotel_13_market_extension/south_mexico/build_results.py` to reproduce the frozen summaries without network access. Validation checks source-file hashes, unique raw IDs, hotel-type counts and both Mexico table reconciliations. No rooms are manufactured from licenses, no borough/category totals are double-counted, and no stock-times-occupancy calculation is presented as measured total nights or Airbnb production.
