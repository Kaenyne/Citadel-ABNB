# Original 13 markets: Barcelona, Rome and Sydney extension

The three primary city-capacity anchors are retained with explicit definitions. This extension adds a large Rome property-candidate frame and a Sydney development pipeline; none measures Airbnb bookings or verifies independent ownership.

| Market | Primary capacity reference | New records obtained | Important scope |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Barcelona | 40,166 rooms, September 2024 | 7 category and 10 district aggregate rows | Municipality; hotels **and hotel-apartments**; 447 establishments, 78,353 beds are distinct units |
| Rome | 48,747 hotel rooms, year-end 2024 | 1,008 hotel registry rows → 1,006 candidate clusters, June 2025 | Roma Capitale; dependencies excluded; active status and remaining aliases unresolved |
| Sydney | 24,990 hotel-category rooms, June 2025 | 69 development applications at 67 addresses, June 2026 | City of Sydney LGA, not Greater Sydney; applications are not operating-property records |

Barcelona's tourism-board census is provisional monthly data. Both the category and district extracts independently reconcile to 447 establishments, 40,166 rooms and 78,353 beds; they are overlapping partitions of the same stock. The wider Barcelonès total is excluded. The municipal hotel-registry page failed and one API fallback returned HTTP 500, so no named city-wide property frame was obtained. [Existing HOTEL-BCN report](https://tdbnews.barcelonaturisme.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2409_Oferta-hotelera.pdf)

Rome's downloaded municipal registry contains 33,660 accommodation records. Filtering exactly `Alberghi` leaves 1,008 unique IDs and 49,596 reported rooms. Two candidate duplicate pairs have matching names after removal of generic Hotel/Albergo terms, the same address, and identical room/bed counts. Collapsing them gives **1,006 candidates and 49,577 known rooms**. Two candidates have missing room counts, preserved as null. There are 897 normalized addresses; 168 candidates share 59 addresses. These require review because separate hotels can occupy the same building and additional aliases may remain. The 80 dependency records and their 657 rooms are excluded. This candidate frame is useful for matching hotel supply to Airbnb, but its difference from the 2024 aggregate is not proved net new supply. [June 2025 municipal CSV](https://dati.comune.roma.it/catalog/dataset/c71fb0bc-109d-4f36-81c0-287ec9bda520/resource/9a1c0c01-0953-4436-89a8-ac286d58dc5c/download/suar2025-06.csv)

Sydney's public development map has a source extraction date of **30 June 2026**. The service count and downloaded rows both equal 69, with unique application IDs. The uncompleted pipeline contains **2,102 hotel-room contributions under construction**, **4,430 approved** and **90 lodged**. A further 21 completed applications represent 1,889 historical room contributions that overlap existing stock. Of the pipeline, 26 applications and 2,670 room contributions have expected completion in FY2026/27; dates can slip and that fiscal year is not exactly the next 12 months from September 2026. Refurbishments and additions may report only the room increase, not the property's total capacity. [Official development service](https://services1.arcgis.com/cNVyNtjGVZybOQWZ/arcgis/rest/services/Development_Monitoring/FeatureServer/0)

Sydney's primary stock category includes standard hotels, B&Bs, pubs and motels; serviced-apartment units and backpacker beds are separate. The city report explicitly excludes Airbnb accommodation. The new pipeline helps constrain potential future supply, but an Airbnb revenue model still needs adoption, allocated rooms, realized channel room-nights and net commissions. [Existing HOTEL-SYD report, printed pp. 4–5 and 9](https://www.cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/-/media/corporate/files/publications/research-and-reports/city-monitor-reports/visitor-accommodation-monitor-june-2025.pdf?download=true)

The three headline sources were already in the team audit and are labeled reused. Rome's newer registry shares municipal lineage with the earlier aggregate; the Sydney map is linked by the existing city report. New property extraction does not establish independent corroboration. The checked local research and saved team-review inventory had no earlier match for the two new dataset URLs; parent integration handles updated remote-team attribution.

The same folder contains source responses, hashes, filtered rows, duplicate lineage, shared-address review, and reproducible fetch/build scripts. Source ledger: `research/sources/hotel_13_europe_sydney_sources.json`. All independent-room fields remain null; actual Airbnb booking observations obtained: **zero**. Previous London and other workstream outputs were not changed.
