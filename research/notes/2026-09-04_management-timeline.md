## Read-through

- **Pattern:** every 2026 hire is from a scaled marketplace/OTA or a frontier AI lab (Booking ×2, Viator, Uber, Meta). Airbnb is staffing to run hotels like an OTA and product like an AI company.
- **Turnover:** CTO (Dec 2025), Head of Ops (Mar 2026), Director of Hotels (May 2026), CBO (announced Sep 2026, effective YE). Balogh and Stephenson were the two longest-tenured non-founder executives.
- **Incentives:** Chesky's award hurdles ($125–$485) and 2030 horizon tie him to the stock, but the outside AI lab and Gebbia's government role leave founder operating bandwidth to Chesky + Blecharczyk.
- **Insider flow:** consistent programmatic selling by Blecharczyk and Gebbia; no open-market buying found in the last 12 months.
