## What the Airbnb observations cover

Filtering the previously collected hotel pages to the approved 13 yields **113 page captures and 111 property-name/address clusters**, across New York City, Paris, London and Rome. These are the existing captures reused, not additional bookings or a representative 13-market hotel census. The other nine markets remain in the table with zero *captured pages in this particular collection*; that is not evidence Airbnb has no hotels there.

The accepted registry links within scope are **26 Paris properties / 1,060 registered rooms**. Room allocation to Airbnb, availability for requested dates, first booking dates, completed nights, fees and hotel-level revenue remain unobserved. Multiple listing IDs are clustered by property identity. Existing captures outside the approved scope remain in their original files but are excluded from this scoped view. [Scoped page observations](../../data/processed/hotel_13_market_panel/airbnb_page_observations_scoped.csv), [property clusters](../../data/processed/hotel_13_market_panel/airbnb_property_clusters_scoped.csv), [registry matches](../../data/processed/hotel_13_market_panel/airbnb_registry_crosswalk_scoped.csv)

The statistically meaningful next dataset is actual channel production or matched receipts with stable hotel IDs, hotel/home classification, cancellations, stay dates, room quantities and provider coverage. Supply registries cannot establish demand uplift or statistical significance. More regions do not fix that missing outcome.

## Independent/boutique capacity and the next-year financial test

The prior two historical independent-room anchors remain in a [separate context table](../../data/processed/hotel_13_market_panel/historical_independent_room_context.csv): CoStar's roughly 38,000 NYC independent rooms in September 2024, and HVS's approximately 51,040 Paris rooms calculated from 92,800 rooms × 55% at year-end 2023. Both are reused, dated source populations. Their shares are not applied to the new registries, and independence does not by itself establish boutique positioning or Airbnb eligibility. [CoStar public abstract](https://www.costar.com/article/1020852869/new-york-citys-existing-supply-and-pipeline-is-flooded-with-independent-hotels), [HVS Paris](https://hvs.com/Print/Paris-Market-Pulse-2024-Going-for-Gold?id=9930)

For each selected market, the required revenue bridge remains: eligible hotels → actual activation → Airbnb-allocated room nights → completed hotel nights → recognized platform revenue, with promotion costs, own-home/HotelTonight displacement and what is already in broker forecasts handled separately. A hotel booking shifted from another OTA can add Airbnb revenue even if the hotel's total occupancy does not rise. No hotel stock total is a substitute for these conversion and economic inputs.

The [Bloomberg/Refinitiv request](../../data/requests/hotel_channel_and_consensus_request.md) and [production schema](../../data/requests/hotel_channel_panel_schema.json) now explicitly use these 13 destinations. The [13-row provider coverage request](../../data/processed/hotel_13_market_panel/provider_coverage_request_13.csv) keeps every market, including absent provider coverage. Consolidated company estimates remain company estimates, not artificially constructed city-level consensus. The prior [financial materiality work](2026-09-07_hotel-expanded-research-results.md) remains the reference for the earnings and expectation test.

## Market-specific limitations

- **Austin:** Includes motel/B&B/hostel/HOA categories; all-hotel city/metro completeness and independence unverified.
- **Barcelona:** Provisional monthly census includes hotels and hotel-apartments; 447 establishments and 78353 beds are separate units. No independent/boutique or Airbnb filter. Official city property-registry landing failed and API fallback returned HTTP500; no city-wide named property frame obtained. September 2024 is the observation date; not September 2026 stock.
- **Chicago:** Hotel business activity676 means seven or more sleeping rooms; room capacity is not a dataset field. 226 returned active-view rows include11 future-start license terms. Current-term filter yields215 license numbers,214 account-site pairs,209 exact addresses. These units are not interchangeable. An account-site can combine multiple brands; separate accounts can share addresses. No physical-property census or operational availability is inferred. No verified ownership/brand independence or boutique flag; no Airbnb matching, allocation or booking inference. The historical Hotel license code1370 returned1402 terms ending by2015 and was excluded from current supply; modern hotel records use Regulated Business License activity676.
- **London:** Includes B&Bs, guest houses, apart-hotels and hostels; July 2026 publication is not a 2026 observation.
- **Los Angeles:** Official TMD directory subset, not all City of Los Angeles hotels; July2024 district plan applies to public-occupancy lodging with50+rooms in city boundaries. Smaller boutique hotels are structurally omitted. 173 distinct directory URLs;172 explicit Total Rooms fields; Bonnie Lee Inn missing. Rooms are undated profile facts retrieved2026-09-07, not audited current operating capacity. 35,501 is the arithmetic sum of reported profile fields. Three shared-address brand pairs are retained; physical room overlap has not been independently reconciled. Postal address place names differ from municipal jurisdiction. City membership follows the TMD directory/plan, not a naive Los Angeles postal-name filter. No verified independent/boutique classification, Airbnb eligibility, contracted allocation, availability or booking inference.
- **Mexico City:** No source identifies boutique eligibility, chain/franchise affiliation or independent-room totals. Supply and licenses do not measure Airbnb listings, activated inventory, room allocation, bookings or incremental hotel demand. 850 is an agency aggregate, not 850 downloaded property records. Same stock appears in category, borough and headline tables; do not sum those populations. Source discloses catalog refresh; historical count increases include previously uncounted hotels. Earlier years not directly comparable. Direct CDMX open-data registry and Secretariat files timed out. Congress-hosted PDF was downloaded; no property-level room registry obtained. Replaces inherited 66,000 search-snippet capacity. Other unverified official-index statements 62,280/62,729 conflict; not averaged or used as additional stock.
- **Nashville:** No source identifies boutique eligibility, chain/franchise affiliation or independent-room totals. Supply and licenses do not measure Airbnb listings, activated inventory, room allocation, bookings or incremental hotel demand. Reused undated-stock county number on September 2025 page; no complete hotel operating registry downloaded. Inspection portal returned 403.
- **New Orleans:** No source identifies boutique eligibility, chain/franchise affiliation or independent-room totals. Supply and licenses do not measure Airbnb listings, activated inventory, room allocation, bookings or incremental hotel demand. Room anchor is rounded, partial-area and not matched to citywide business rows. Downloaded CSV has 391 lodging business rows, of which 262 hotel/motel rows map to 243 exact addresses; duplicates include operator changes, dual brands and bar/garage registrations. No unique physical-hotel total inferred. CSV last rows update July 16, 2024. Monthly refresh label does not make it current; no rooms field. Newer March 2025 study uses another city administrative system; differences cannot establish exits or supply change.
- **New York City:** No room-count field; active/renewal status is not evidence of Airbnb operation or booking production.
- **Paris:** Classified hotels only; chain affiliation and operating/Airbnb status unverified; unclassified stock excluded.
- **Rome:** Excludes separately reported 657 rooms in hotel dependencies. No matched chain/independent classification. Newer registry is not proven complete active supply; primary city aggregate retained pending alias/status reconciliation. No Airbnb listings, allocation, bookings or revenue identified.
- **San Diego:** City room count is40158; county68954 and downtown14878 are nested or broader scopes and must not be added. Fact sheet dated2026-04-30 does not provide a separate infrastructure census date; not a September2026 hotel-by-hotel count. 681 includes hotels, motels, bed-and-breakfasts and casino hotels in regional infrastructure; it is not a City of San Diego hotel-only property count. Tax-account file has59419 active business records;52 are coded72111 hotel/motel and524 use721 accommodation-prefix codes. These are not additional hotels: corporate addresses, combined products and classification gaps prevent a census interpretation. Independent rooms, boutique rooms, Airbnb supply and Airbnb hotel nights remain unobserved.
- **Sydney:** City definition includes standard hotels, B&Bs, pubs and motels; excludes separate serviced apartment units and backpacker beds. No complete operating-hotel property register obtained; 69 development applications are a distinct dataset. City report explicitly does not cover Airbnb accommodation. Independent ownership and Airbnb uptake remain unknown.
