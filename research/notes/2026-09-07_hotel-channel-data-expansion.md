# Airbnb hotel channel data expansion — independent provider audit

As of September 7, 2026. This expands, and does not constitute a second independent replication of, `2026-09-07_hotel-demand-independent-review.md`. It adds provider evidence and access checks. It does not alter the 13/25 market definitions or add markets to the existing listing panel.

**Result:** actual hotel-channel transactions exist at scale, but this audit obtained **no public absolute Airbnb hotel room-night, booking-value, or Airbnb revenue series** with a verified hotel-only denominator. The best public Airbnb-specific production observation remains Cloudbeds' +4.1% room-night growth in January–July 2025. HOTREC adds a small historical OTA-share observation. SiteMinder adds channel revenue rankings. None identifies the all-channel occupancy uplift caused by a hotel joining Airbnb.

The most promising new route without collecting data separately from every hotel is an entitled provider extract: either PMS/channel-manager production or consumer receipts with a verified hotel match. A current Measurable AI travel panel and a public generic travel data dictionary make the receipt route concrete. No account, purchase, outreach, or download form was submitted.

## The concrete receipt-data opportunity

Measurable AI's August 2026 article proves that an actual travel receipt panel is being analyzed now. Hong Kong describes **consumer origin**, not hotel destination; the analysis tracks trips to multiple destinations. The article consolidates multiple bookings into trips. That operation is unsuitable for summing hotel room nights unless the underlying reservations remain available. It does not publish the Airbnb subgroup, unique Airbnb hotel count, classification rules, HotelTonight separation, or cancellation/refund completeness. Its closing platform list also omits Airbnb although the methodology names it; the Airbnb inclusion must be confirmed in the delivered extract. [Measurable AI](https://blog.measurable.ai/2026/08/05/mainland-china-overtakes-japan-hong-kong-travel-data/)

A vendor-authored **generic travel** listing has a public data dictionary with receipt/update IDs, pseudonymous account IDs, order type, prices/currencies, hotel name/address/coordinates, check-in/out, room count, night count, and room type. Its example is explicitly **Agoda**, so it does not verify Airbnb field completeness. One displayed example has check-out 86,400 seconds after check-in but `numOfNights=0`; hotelId is blank. Dates and identifiers require validation before aggregation. The vendor describes cancellation emails, which does not establish linked refunds or completed stays. The catalog's prose says 23+ countries while its country list says 48: request separate origin/destination and platform coverage. [Measurable AI's travel catalog](https://datarade.ai/data-products/transactional-e-receipt-data-hotel-travel-hospitality-measurable-ai)

The provider's 2024Q1 coverage PDF has Airbnb check marks across 22 of its displayed 26 country columns, including the U.S. This is a historical coverage map, without sample counts or hotel classification; it must not be substituted for the project's market universe. Its Bloomberg Enterprise Access Point footnote appears **inside the in-app-purchase column**, not the travel column. It confirms a provider distribution relationship for that dataset only. Airbnb/travel availability through Bloomberg and the user's entitlement remain unverified; no Refinitiv distribution relationship was verified. [Coverage PDF, pp. 11 and 13](https://file.measurable.ai/coverage.pdf)

A candidate **SAMPLE Airbnb Transactional E-receipt Data** listing exists via a secondary catalog link to [Databricks Marketplace](https://marketplace.databricks.com/details/443917ec-8334-4e4b-b43c-3bc62bb56b8c/Measurable-AI_-SAMPLE-Airbnb-Transactional-E-receipt-Data-Hotel-Travel-Hospitality). The public HTML returned only an application shell; no raw sample or Airbnb data dictionary was obtained. Browser control reported no browser available. The secondary catalog's generic credit-card explanation is not a valid description of this email-receipt dataset. The generic Agoda schema is the strongest schema evidence actually read.

**First entitled export to request:** month × consumer-origin-country × hotel-destination-market × storefront, with raw and constant-consumer counts of receipts, unique reservations, gross and uncancelled reservations, reserved room nights, booking value, unique consumers, matched physical hotels, classified hotel/home/unknown observations, and HotelTonight/unknown-origin observations. Include null rates, parser version, geography definitions, earliest reliable date, revision history, and weights. These aggregated diagnostics determine whether a full receipt feed is useful before processing it. For NYC, require NYC-destination counts explicitly; a large global/Hong Kong panel does not guarantee useful NYC coverage.

ADR declined 8% to a reported US$289. The parenthetical amounts are not clearly labeled as levels versus changes; exact comparison dates are absent. ## Scalable export specification

This is a requested schema, not data already obtained. It supports hundreds or thousands of properties without confusing repeated observations with independent hotels.

1. **Property master:** provider property ID; stable physical-hotel ID; brand/group ID; address/geocode; destination market from the frozen project mapping; hotel/inn/B&B/hostel/apartment/unknown classification and evidence; independent/chain definition; operating rooms; opening/closure dates; provider coverage start/end; Airbnb contract, first-bookable and first-booking dates separately; old/new hotel product and HotelTonight connection history.
2. **Reservation version table:** pseudonymous provider reservation ID and upstream reservation ID; property ID; creation, change, cancellation and stay dates; status; originating consumer storefront; raw receiving channel; room type; rooms per reservation; actual room nights; gross price, tax, fees, discounts, refunds, currency, hotel net receipt, channel commission; ingestion timestamp and version. Personal guest names/emails are unnecessary.
3. **Hotel × stay-date × channel aggregate:** completed room nights; gross/net room revenue; reservations; cancellations; no-shows; ADR numerator and denominator. Keep all channels, including direct/walk-in/GDS and HotelTonight. Sum deduplicated reservation facts into this table, not both tables into totals.
4. **Hotel × stay-date inventory:** operating rooms, out-of-order rooms, sellable rooms, Airbnb allotment/bookable inventory, restriction/stop-sell dates, and extract-as-of time. Shared pools across channels must remain one physical inventory total.
5. **Coverage diagnostics:** hotel counts and properties continuously present by period; new/removed providers; missing source labels; known/unknown storefront splits; observed versus modeled fields; inclusion probability or provider weights if available; booking-to-stay reconciliation; currency policy; left/right censoring.

A single provider extract with property-level keys can be analyzed first. A second provider should be joined through the physical-hotel crosswalk and reservation lineage; it should not simply be appended as extra evidence. Mews/SiteMinder routing, Cloudbeds/Demand360 integration, reused Cloudbeds reports, D-EDGE regional publications, and syndications of a vendor press release are explicit overlap risks.

The receipt alternative additionally needs panel membership/active intervals, stable pseudonymous consumer IDs, receipt-to-reservation deduplication, and classification confidence. Its panel bookings are a sample total, not global bookings. Scaling them requires independently validated coverage weights specific to hotel bookings and destinations, with sensitivity to unmatched properties and consumer churn.

## Translating production into the investment question

For actual sample data, report Airbnb hotel room nights and hotel room revenue directly, alongside those same hotels' all-channel room nights, RevPAR, net acquisition cost, and capacity. Separate existing hotels from newly bookable hotels; distinguish hotels newly appearing in the provider from hotels newly joining Airbnb.

Hotel room revenue is not Airbnb revenue. Airbnb's recognized hotel revenue requires actual commission/fee terms, cancellation treatment, taxes, incentives and revenue-recognition timing. A generic corporate take rate or home-host fee multiplied by hotel room revenue is a scenario, not an observed result. Similarly, more Airbnb hotel nights can benefit Airbnb while leaving hotel total occupancy unchanged because demand moved from another OTA.

The public provider evidence establishes that Airbnb already produces hotel-channel business. It does not establish a huge all-channel demand influx for adopting hotels, nor does it quantify current global hotel nights or contribution. The next useful evidence is an entitled production/receipt coverage export, not additional listing snapshots or more copies of the same vendor report.
