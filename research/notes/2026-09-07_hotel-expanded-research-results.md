## 1. Absolute hotel nights and Airbnb revenue

The [full disclosure audit](2026-09-07_hotel-absolute-disclosure-search.md) inspected earnings materials and acquisition notes. HotelTonight's $441.357 million acquisition consideration is a purchase price, **not revenue**. The notes do not supply a separate numeric revenue contribution that solves the current denominator. [2020 10-K, Note 6](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1559720/000155972021000010/airbnb-10k.htm)

There is one loose conditional bound: if the single-digit share uses the same Q2 period and consistent lodging-night definition, hotel nights are below 10% of 148.3 million Nights and Seats Booked, or **below 14.83 million**. Seats make this conservative; the definitions are not reconciled. It is not a point estimate, physical room-night count or a defensible annual revenue base. Do not annualize or monetize it using Airbnb's blended ADR and take rate. [Q2 letter](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/v2/Airbnb-Q2-2026-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)

An independent [McCarthy/Wilbur study](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kennethcwilbur/website/master/mccarthy-wilbur%20wp%202023%20did%20recent%20platform%20mergers%20reduce%20competition.pdf) uses **2.23 million U.S. consumers, January 2016–May 2022**, and separates HotelTonight merchant activity. This verifies that a legacy-channel panel is possible. It does not classify hotels inside Airbnb or provide current global hotel production. Its paying-customer shares and trimmed average payments must not be multiplied into an invented revenue estimate.

The first Bloomberg/Refinitiv target is therefore original broker hotel models and complete management remarks, with every number labeled disclosed actual, estimated historical base, forecast or scenario. One specific lead is the **June 5, 2026 Jefferies hotel model**, reportedly targeting roughly $1 billion annual hotel revenue by 2030. That public summary is a forecast lead, not a verified current actual or consensus. [Public report lead](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/airbnb-hotels-experiences-push-could-153900298.html)

## 2. Are hotels getting a large influx of demand?

The strongest available public hotel-channel observation is Cloudbeds' **Airbnb room nights +4.1%, ADR −2.0% and average stay −3.4%** in January–July 2025 versus 2024. It covers mixed independent lodging, does not disclose the Airbnb cohort size, and predates the 2026 rollout. It cannot confirm or contradict current growth on a comparable basis. [Cloudbeds](https://www.cloudbeds.com/articles/market-pulse/)

HOTREC reports Airbnb at **1.0% of relative OTA share**, for reference year 2023, using unweighted hotel survey responses. The complete study covers 3,089 properties, but subgroup table labels differ. Do not multiply that 1% by European hotel nights: the OTA denominator, property weighting and underlying populations do not align. [HOTREC full report, p. 104](https://www.hotrec.eu/media/static/files/import/all_news_2024_2024_21/hotrec-distribution-study-2024.pdf)

SiteMinder reports Airbnb ranked **ninth in France and tenth in Spain** by channel revenue for 2025. D-EDGE reports a historical **20-fold channel-share increase over five years** in its 2021 report. These are useful evidence of distribution, but neither yields current absolute bookings. Large provider-wide booking totals are not Airbnb hotel sample sizes. [SiteMinder France](https://www.siteminder.com/fr/actualite/hotel-booking-trends-france-2026/), [Spain](https://www.siteminder.com/es/noticias/hotel-booking-trends-2026/), [D-EDGE](https://www.d-edge.com/hotel-distribution-and-the-pandemic-the-travel-chessboard/)

**The strongest newly identified scalable data lead:** Measurable AI describes roughly **200,000 Hong Kong-consumer travel receipts across six platforms including Airbnb, 2019–2026**. Hong Kong is consumer origin; destination coverage is broader. The Airbnb subgroup, hotel/home classifier and NYC counts are unpublished. Its generic public travel schema is an Agoda example, not proof that Airbnb receipts contain those fields. No raw feed or entitlement was obtained. Its Bloomberg coverage footnote applies to in-app purchases, so travel availability through Bloomberg remains unverified. [Current provider analysis](https://blog.measurable.ai/2026/08/05/mainland-china-overtakes-japan-hong-kong-travel-data/), [coverage document](https://file.measurable.ai/coverage.pdf)

For the stock thesis, distinguish two tests. **Airbnb-channel production** measures business Airbnb earns. **Hotel all-channel occupancy uplift** asks whether joining Airbnb increases the hotel's overall demand. A booking diverted from a competing OTA can help Airbnb even if the hotel's occupancy is unchanged. Conversely, a booking diverted from Airbnb's own homes or HotelTonight may contribute little net company growth. We need the first test to quantify revenue; the second helps explain hotel adoption and retention. Neither is identified by page counts.

The [provider memo](2026-09-07_hotel-channel-data-expansion.md) specifies production exports, receipt diagnostics, storefront/HotelTonight reconciliation and access limitations.

## 3. Can acquisition and supply scale in balance?

The economically useful funnel is **eligible property → contract → first bookable date → allocated room nights → first booking → sustained room-night production → net platform revenue/contribution**. Official registries cover the first stage only partly; live page captures identify presentation, not the booking or revenue stages.

In NYC, management disclosed **more than 100 hotels / more than 20,000 rooms** on the site in Q4 2025. That is a stronger issuer supply anchor than our incomplete 54-property NYC page sample. Neither number states how many rooms Airbnb receives each night. [Q4 2025 official transcript, pp. 17–18](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Airbnb-Q4-25-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf)

With a production feed, measure each monthly activation cohort's 30/60/90-day booking conversion, completed room nights per **available Airbnb room-night**, booking value, cancellations and survival. Separately track existing hotels' production, new hotels' ramp and exits. If new supply grows faster than demand while mature-cohort productivity and retention fall, acquisition is outrunning monetization. Stable productivity with growing allocated inventory would support a measured expansion. Neither condition has been observed yet.

For NYC, stratify by neighborhood, property size, price segment and travel dates; retain weekday/weekend and event effects. Compare markets or hotels with similar pre-period trends and calendar shocks. Regulation can motivate a hypothesis about marginal value, but the legal regime itself is not a measured causal uplift. A hotel first receiving a 2025 city license is not necessarily newly opened or newly listed on Airbnb.

The global TAM should report rooms in **verified independent**, **verified chain-affiliated**, and **unknown** properties separately, with boutique positioning as a separate classification. Do not use all 606,429 French classified rooms as independent TAM, or treat the 73.8% of Paris properties below 50 rooms as 73.8% of rooms. That small-property subset accounts for **43.5% of Paris classified rooms**. This is a concrete capacity result, with ownership still missing.

## 4. Revenue, earnings and expectations: what would move the pitch?

| Target | Evidence needed | Calculation and pitch standard |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Completed hotel room nights, room booking value, actual commissions/fees, cancellations, revenue timing; existing hotel base | Reconcile to platform recognized revenue. Compare the revised hotel component with the hotel component already embedded in the company forecast; subtract own-platform displacement. A gross hotel-room sales number is not Airbnb revenue. |
| Earnings | Revenue above baseline, promotion eligibility/redemption/funding, variable servicing/acquisition costs and incremental fixed spend | Incremental EBITDA equals incremental recognized revenue less incremental operating costs. Preserve accounting classification; do not subtract credits twice if already netted from revenue. GAAP EPS also needs SBC, taxes and share count. |
| Guidance / investor expectations | Timestamped company guidance and broker forecasts, hotel assumptions, revisions after hotel disclosures | Measure the hotel-driven forecast change against the existing baseline, its dispersion and historical misses. A large hotel business already expected is not automatically upside. Aggregate revenue/guidance revisions alone cannot be attributed to hotels. |

The currently visible public company forecast is **FY2026 revenue $14.16 billion and FY2027 $15.76 billion**, attributed to S&P Global and last updated September 3. This is a public annual proxy, not our terminal extraction or a hotel estimate. [Forecast source](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/abnb/forecast/)

For scale only, **1% of FY2027 revenue is $157.6 million**. At an explicitly hypothetical $20 of incremental recognized Airbnb revenue per extra hotel night, that would require **7.88 million additional nights above the baseline**, before own-platform displacement. A 0.5%/2% hurdle is 3.94m/15.76m nights at that assumption. These are reverse-engineered requirements, not hotel forecasts; FY2027 is not the requested four-quarter period. Actual fee and current terminal inputs must replace the assumptions. [Saved arithmetic and baseline](../../data/processed/hotel_expanded_research/expectations_coverage.json)

Current guidance already incorporates a broad growth outlook: Q3 revenue **$4.69–4.77 billion**, FY2026 revenue growth **at least mid-teens** and adjusted EBITDA margin **at least 35.5%**. The public guidance does not isolate hotels. [Q2 letter, p. 7](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/v2/Airbnb-Q2-2026-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)

Promotions can materially alter contribution. The independently checked 27-case sensitivity grid uses room booking value **excluding taxes/fees**, not Airbnb's reported GBV. In one illustrative case, $1 billion × 12% fees = $120 million; 15% credit on all eligible value × 50% redemption costs $75 million if Airbnb funds all of it and caps do not bind. That leaves $45 million fees minus expected credits and $30 million after an assumed $15 million other variable cost. This is **cohort lifetime economics, not same-quarter GAAP revenue**. The contract rate, exposure, funding and redemption remain assumptions. [Scenario data](../../data/processed/hotel_rollout_economics/promotion_economics_sensitivity.csv), [official credit terms](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/4132), [supplier terms](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3265)

Management's reported 35% subsequent home-booking rate is a one-year cohort measure, not proof of incremental home demand. The cohort predates the summer 2026 credit product. A usable cross-sell estimate needs comparable users, baseline return propensity, timing and incentive costs. [Q2 letter, p. 5 footnote](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/v2/Airbnb-Q2-2026-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)

## 5. How much data would support a statistical conclusion?

**We have enough registry records to build large sampling frames, but no production panel on which to estimate demand uplift or its standard error.** No p-value or achieved statistical power is reported.

For planning only, a two-group, two-sided 5% test with 80% power, equal residual variances and independent hotel outcomes gives these approximate sample requirements when the residual standard deviation equals the mean:

| Minimum relative difference worth detecting | Hotels per group | Total independent hotels |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 20% | 393 | 786 |
| 10% | 1,570 | 3,140 |
| 5% | 6,280 | 12,560 |

For a 10% difference, reducing the residual coefficient of variation to 0.5 gives 393 per group; increasing it to 1.5 gives 3,532. These are transparent normal-approximation calculations, **not a prescription that 3,140 arbitrary hotel records produce causality**. Repeated dates are correlated; shared operators, cities and shocks matter. A staggered-adoption design needs observed pre-period variance, explicit controls and design-specific power simulation. [Planning calculation and assumptions](../../data/processed/hotel_expanded_research/statistical_design.json), [NIST sample-size principles](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc222.htm)

A precise forecast can be useful without proving all-channel hotel uplift. Its uncertainty must include provider selection, hotel classification, coverage weights, commissions and displacement—not only sampling error. A million biased receipts do not become a global hotel census. For revenue materiality, translate the minimum economically relevant dollar revision into a required room-night effect before choosing the sample target.

On the stock-reaction side, the team has **23 earnings events**, with revenue consensus for all 23 but adjusted EBITDA consensus for only eight. Daily broker-history exports improve timing and definition; they do not turn 23 company events into thousands of independent earnings observations. An event model should remain parsimonious, separate pandemic regimes, and avoid calling a hotel comment causal when guidance, FX, core nights and margins changed simultaneously.
