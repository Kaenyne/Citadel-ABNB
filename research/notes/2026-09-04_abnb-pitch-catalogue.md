# ABNB pitch catalogue (Sep 2025 – Sep 2026, plus earlier context)

**What this is:** every public long, short or neutral write-up on Airbnb we could find across Substack, sell-side notes, university reports and X (Twitter), with the date, stance, valuation method, key numbers and a quality rating. Use the table of contents to jump to any entry.
**Compiled:** 2026-09-04 (updated 2026-09-05). Author: Krishang, with Claude Code.
**Not covered:** Value Investors Club and AlphaSense (both need a logged-in session that was not available; VIC's public index shows no ABNB write-up). MBI Deep Dives, Rebound Capital and Speedwell are paywalled, so only their public portions are summarised.

**Quality rating (1–10)** is a judgment on how useful the piece is for our pitch: depth of numbers, rigor of the valuation, whether it takes a differentiated view, and how much of it we could actually read. 8+ is worth reading in full; 5–7 has one or two useful ideas; below 5 is context only.

**Stock context when compiled:** ~$182–187, ~$108B market cap, +44% YoY, 4-year high after the Q2'26 print (+17% on 7 Aug 2026). Street: ~23 Buy / ~20 Hold / 2 Sell; PT range $125 (Morgan Stanley) to $220 (Rosenblatt).

### MBI Deep Dives — "2Q'26: Widening the Gap with Booking" (2026-08-07) and "Airbnb Model Update (2026)" (2026-08-19)
- **Stance:** Long; re-underwriting after a ~50% move since Oct 2025. **Rating: 7 / 6** (paid; only public portion read).
- **Method:** Full financial model (paid).
- **Key numbers:** Nights +10%, best since 3Q'23; LTM take rate down ~31 bps YoY despite insurance revenue +60%; NA ADR ex-FX +7%; app 64% of nights; first-time bookers +11%.
- **Argument:** Acceleration is sustained and core markets moved from low-single-digit to high-single-digit nights growth. Take-rate recovery is "more sluggish than expected" because RNPL is ~20% of GBV and incentives for hotels/experiences/services offset the fee changes.
- **Why it matters for us:** The cleanest public statement that take rate, not nights, is the swing factor for 2026–27.
- Links: https://www.mbi-deepdives.com/abnb2q26/ · https://mbideepdives.substack.com/p/airbnb-model-update-2026

### Summit Stocks (Lucas) — "Airbnb Is Building a Travel Ecosystem, Not a Booking App" (2026-05-27)
- **Stance:** Long, adding. **Rating: 6.**

- **Key numbers:** Q1'26 revenue $2.7B (+18%; +15% ex-FX), GBV $29.2B (+19%), nights 156.2M (+9%), app 63% of nights, RNPL ~20% of GBV, hotel-to-home conversion 55%, cash $12.1B, debt $2.5B, Q1 buyback $1.1B.

<a id="phaetrix-2026-03-09"></a>
### Phaetrix — "Airbnb Says AI Is Working. I Want to See the Bill." (2026-03-09)
- **Stance:** Watch, medium conviction; not a Buy. **Rating: 7.**
- **Method:** Forward P/E mid-20s to 30x; EV/FCF 15–17x; explicit tripwires (add on weakness to low $120s with monetisation signal; exit below $110 on GBV deceleration).
- **Key numbers:** Q4'25 revenue $2.8B (+12%), GBV $20.4B (+16%), nights +10%, implied take rate 13.6% vs 14.1% YoY; AI resolves ~1/3 of support tickets; RNPL >70% adoption of eligible US bookings.
- **Argument:** AI is cost leverage, not monetisation; "adoption ≠ take-rate expansion"; needs proof by late 2026 or the stock is dead money. The take-rate decline is the "critical red flag."
- Link: https://phaetrix.substack.com/p/abnb-abnb-airbnbs-ai-pivot-is-priced

<a id="longyield-2026-02-25"></a>
### LongYield — "Airbnb's Illusion of Strength: Record Cash Flow, Weakening Foundation" (2026-02-25)
- **Stance:** Fairly valued with asymmetric downside. **Rating: 8** (best public bear framework).
- **Method:** EV/EBITDA (26x vs Booking 14–18x) and scenarios: base $132, bull $175, bear $88 vs $128 price.
- **Key numbers:** FY25 revenue $12.2B (+10.3%), nights 533M (+8.4%), GBV $91.3B (+11.6%), FCF $4.6B (38%); SBC $1.4B (12.7% of revenue) so SBC-adjusted FCF margin ~25%; US host occupancy 57% (2024) → 50% (spring 2025) while US listings hit 1.76M; NYC Local Law 18 cut supply >90% with hotel ADR +6%; Barcelona phase-out by 2028, Paris 90-day cap, Amsterdam 30 nights, Berlin €500k fines; Guest Favorites ~50% of Q4 bookings; 550k listings removed.
- **Argument:** Cash flow masks deteriorating host economics, spreading regulation, Google Travel as a distribution chokepoint, and quality curation concentrating supply among professionals. Guest float inflates "net cash."
- Link: https://longyield.substack.com/p/airbnbs-illusion-of-strength-record

## F. What the catalogue says in aggregate

**KPIs people underwrite, ranked:** (1) nights & seats growth; (2) GBV and ADR; (3) take rate (most contested: 13.2% flat, LTM −31 bps, guided flat for 2026; bulls model 15.5%, bears see an incentive treadmill and the 6–10% host-fee pilot); (4) adj. EBITDA and FCF margin, always SBC-adjusted by skeptics; (5) S&M growing 1.5x revenue; (6) first-time bookers; (7) app share of nights; (8) hotels (3x growth, 35–55% convert to homes, no unit economics); (9) RNPL (>20% of GBV, +1 pt cancellations, laps Q3'26); (10) Experiences/Services; (11) support cost per booking; (12) regional mix; (13) buybacks and share count; (14) bedroom nights.

**Valuation methods in use:** forward P/E (20x FY27 at Barclays to 33x at Trefis), EV/EBITDA (20x 2027E at Truist vs 25.5x at Bernstein), DCF ($101 Henry Fund to $425 Eremos), EV/FCF and SBC-adjusted FCF yield (15–24x; 4.2% yield), SOTP add-ons for new businesses (+$50–150/share), reverse DCF ("market implies 10.5–11% growth"). The $125–220 PT range is almost entirely a multiple debate on a consensual 10–12% revenue path.

**Live debates:** structural vs borrowed reacceleration (RNPL, fee migration, FX, World Cup); take-rate expansion vs incentives; whether hotels/experiences/services are worth anything; AI as cost lever vs disintermediation; supply and regulation (Morgan Stanley's listings deceleration, NYC, Chicago, Barcelona, Paris); quality of cash (SBC, guest float, S&M); governance and insider selling.
