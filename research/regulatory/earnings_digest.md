# Regulatory evidence from ABNB earnings calls

14 quarterly calls archived: 13 company-hosted PDF transcripts (Q1 2023-Q1 2026) and the Q2 2026 public HTML fallback. The company-hosted PDFs are FactSet CallStreet corrected transcripts. Full text remains in gitignored data/raw. Targeted keyword screening was followed by review of the relevant Q&A/context; this is not a claim that every sentence received legal analysis.

## Most useful passages

### REG-T01 | 2023-Q3 | 2023-11-01

**Speaker:** Brian Chesky; Dave Stephenson · **Locator:** PDF pp. 11-12, 19

Management said 80% of its top 200 markets already had regulation and generally workable rules. It described New York as an outlier and pointed to bookings shifting toward Jersey City.

**Pitch use:** Diversification and recapture are management's defence. Existing regulation does not prevent later tightening.

**Evidence type:** Management commentary; not an independently measured net revenue effect

[Transcript source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2023/q3/Airbnb-Q3-23-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf) · Related: REG-01

### REG-T02 | 2024-Q3 | 2024-11-07

**Speaker:** Brian Chesky · **Locator:** PDF pp. 17-18; Jed Kelly question

Discussed adding hotels in New York and homes in nearby New Jersey, while expressing hope for a workable New York policy solution. Used Paris as a partnership example.

**Pitch use:** Regulation can change Airbnb's supply mix and product priorities. Subsequent Paris tightening shows partnerships do not eliminate risk.

**Evidence type:** Management strategy and opinion

[Transcript source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2024/q3/Airbnb-Q3-24-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf) · Related: REG-01, REG-08

### REG-T03 | 2024-Q4 | 2025-02-13

**Speaker:** Brian Chesky · **Locator:** PDF pp. 17-18; Naved Khan question

Repeated the 80%-of-top-200-markets statistic, described cities increasingly as partners, and argued New York was a cautionary example others would not follow.

**Pitch use:** Test this claim against later multi-city tightening and EU-wide policy. Management's assertions about housing outcomes are not causal research.

**Evidence type:** Management opinion; dated claim

[Transcript source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2024/q4/Airbnb-Q4-24-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf) · Related: REG-01, REG-25

### REG-T04 | 2025-Q1 | 2025-05-01

**Speaker:** Brian Chesky · **Locator:** PDF p. 8

Identified Spain and Italy among international growth markets beyond the five core markets.

**Pitch use:** Restrictions may impair the expansion thesis even where current global revenue exposure is limited.

**Evidence type:** Management growth strategy

[Transcript source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2025/q1/Airbnb-Q1-25-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf) · Related: REG-02, REG-14, REG-22

### REG-T05 | 2025-Q2 | 2025-08-06

**Speaker:** Brian Chesky · **Locator:** PDF pp. 14-15

Described major events as opportunities to add hosts and strengthen relationships with regulators, citing Italy and the coming Winter Olympics.

**Pitch use:** Event partnerships are a mitigation strategy; they are not permanent exemptions from local supply restrictions. Targeted review found no quantified Spain regulatory revenue loss.

**Evidence type:** Management mitigation strategy

[Transcript source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2025/q2/Airbnb-Q2-25-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf) · Related: REG-14

### REG-T06 | 2025-Q3 | 2025-11-06

**Speaker:** Brian Chesky · **Locator:** PDF p. 10

Identified New York and Madrid as markets constrained by regulation and discussed hotel pilots in those markets and Los Angeles.

**Pitch use:** Strongest direct transcript connection between regulation and a commercial response. Model hotel recapture separately from lost home nights.

**Evidence type:** Management acknowledgement of supply constraint

[Transcript source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2025/q3/Airbnb-Q3-25-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf) · Related: REG-01, REG-12

### REG-T08 | 2026-Q1 | 2026-05-07

**Speaker:** Brian Chesky; Ellie Mertz · **Locator:** PDF pp. 4, 9

Management said hotel expansion helps satisfy demand where homes supply is limited by regulation, and described large events as a way to add supply and build city relationships.

**Pitch use:** Hotel substitution is an explicit mitigation, but gross booking recapture need not preserve take rate or contribution margin.

**Evidence type:** Management mitigation strategy

[Transcript source](https://s26.q4cdn.com/656283129/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/Airbnb-Q1-26-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf) · Related: REG-01, REG-12

### REG-T09 | 2026-Q2 | 2026-08-06

**Speaker:** Brian Chesky · **Locator:** Public HTML transcript; question from Richard Clarke (Bernstein) on expansion/ancillary offerings; locate 'policy is a risk'

Chesky characterised policy as an ongoing risk to manage, while identifying AI as existential. The call also discussed hotel growth.

**Pitch use:** Acknowledge persistent policy risk; this statement does not quantify loss or invalidate enacted restrictions.

**Evidence type:** Third-party transcript of management commentary; company PDF retrieval returned 404
