## Thesis 1 (133 words)

**Thesis 1 – RNPL's Lift Is Lapping While Its Cancellations Are Still Arriving:** RNPL ($0 at booking, payment due just before
free cancellation ends), 14-day free cancellation and a host-only fee added ~3pts of nights growth in 1Q26, by management's count
(Exhibit 3, red). RNPL reached ~20% of GBV in 1Q26 and barely moved in its first full global quarter; management then stopped
sizing the lift. RNPL bookings cancel more (2Q26 10-Q), and management's 16%→17% platform rate implies ~22% on RNPL bookings.
Guests pay just before check-in, so about half of those cancellations land a quarter after the booking was counted: 1H26 got the
lift, 2H26 gets the cancellations. They cost ~0.9pt of nights a quarter just as the bundle laps, taking 4Q26 to ~7% in our bear
case against the Street's +9.9%.

*Fixes:* management never said 16%→17% ran "3Q25 to 1H26"; that path is our inference. The draft compared the ~7% bear case (a
4Q26 number) with +11.53% (the 3Q26 Street figure).

## Thesis 2 (125 words)

**Thesis 2 – Nights and ADR Will Print Below Consensus:** Our alt data puts 3Q26 nights at +9.2–9.5%, below every Street
estimate. A stays index built on 71 million Inside Airbnb guest reviews across 123 markets beats a naive forecast out of sample
(0.68x the error), and twelve external series, led by STR hotel RevPAR fading from +8.2% in July to +4% by mid-August, read
+9.2%. The World Cup hid little: host-city stays ran 7.9pts above controls, ~0.1pt of global nights. On price, the Street's 3Q26
ADR ($177.06) matches a straight currency translation ($177.07); our better-tested FX estimate gives $175.66, and ADR slows to +1%
ex-FX by 1Q27 as the bundle's ADR lift laps and North America's slowdown tilts mix to cheaper regions.
